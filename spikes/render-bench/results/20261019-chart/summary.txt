operation but the pointer's tooltip, which is one too. dotnet.native.wasm is 2.9 MB without AOT and
22.2 MB with it.

Beside the proof of concept: its redraw of 1,566 dates took 644 ms on Server and 1.8 s on
WebAssembly. The same redraw here (data replacement) takes 21 ms on Server, 125 ms on WebAssembly
and 87 ms with AOT, at its median; the slowest operation on any host, the return to the whole range
on WebAssembly, takes 119 ms. A pointer move answers within the frame it was made in on all three.

AOT: about 30% off the redraws (replace, narrow, widen), nothing off hiding a series, for a download
7.6 times the size.

The drag waits, untimed, for the tooltip after each return to the whole range: the return draws
anew, and a drag pressed before the pointer entry has that drawing is refused (spine rule 6), which
a script that drags at once meets on the Server host.
