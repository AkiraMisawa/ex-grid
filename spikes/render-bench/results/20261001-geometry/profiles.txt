Ticket 44: the thirteen line styles drawn by the two Excel modes ("Show the thirteen line styles"),
one black box each, read from headless Chrome 154 screenshots at device scale 1 and 1.5 (CDP
device-scale emulation, not browser zoom). Written as the eleventh Windows run's case 9 is
(verification/2026-10-01-windows-excel-11/cell-format.md, group 3): '#' luminance below 64, '='
below 128, '-' below 192, '.' otherwise; a row the line does not touch is left out.

Offsets count from the boundary between the two cells. The bench's gridline is the upper (left)
cell's last pixel, so at scale 1 it is offset -1: thin on -1 is "on the gridline", medium on -2
and -1 is "the gridline and 1 px above", thick on -2..0 is "1 px each side", double on -2 and 0 is
"two lines either side of a white gridline". At 1.5 the right edge's offsets count from a boundary
at a half device pixel, so they read one lower than the bottom edge's.

excel-100.png and excel-150.png are BorderInCellExcel's boxes, enlarged.
