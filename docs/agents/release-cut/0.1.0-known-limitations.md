# `0.1.0`'s Known limitations: the lines drafted for the cut

The cut before a tag writes the version's Release Notes (ADR-0190, ADR-0205), and a stable version's notes carry a
Known limitations block (ADR-0190's note of 2026-10-12: each line begins with the products it reaches in bold, names
the limitation in a sentence, and links the page that says it in full). ADR-0202 lists what the block holds. Each
branch that adds one drafts its line here, so the cut takes them from one place. Not a page of the site and not a
Change Note.

## From `claude/area-corrections` (2026-10-13)

The products are ExSheet's; the cut puts **ExSheet:** before each.

- `NPV` is not supported and is `#NAME?`, as `IRR`, `XIRR` and `RATE` are: its last digits differ from
  Excel's, and no evaluation found gives every result Excel was seen to give
  ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- `PMT`'s payment can differ from Excel's in its last digits: 23 of the 30 payments Excel was asked differ
  from ExSheet's by 1 to 11 units in the last place, a relative difference of at most about 1.3e-15, which
  at two of them shows in the 15th significant digit; and where the evaluations Excel's payments leave
  part, `PMT` is `#VALUE!` ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- `INDIRECT` over a Linked Table's name on its own, `INDIRECT("Positions")`, is `#VALUE!`, where Excel reads
  the table's data: a Linked Table is read by key, never by position
  ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- `INDIRECT`'s R1C1 text with a row or column number past the Sheet's edge, `INDIRECT("R1048577C2",FALSE)`,
  is `#VALUE!` until Excel has been asked whether it names no place or a place
  ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- A Formula ExSheet refused whose array's shape could depend on a Value, such as a refused `FILTER` or
  `=LEN(A1:A3)`, spills nothing, and the cells its array would cover read as blank
  ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).

## From `claude/dod-gaps-restatements` (#95, 2026-10-13)

- **ExGrid:** behind a remote browser isolation service, the repaint after Ctrl+↓, Ctrl+↑ or PageDown is not checked: its browser test passes, but the check behind such a service was not run for this version ([Behind a remote browser isolation service](getting-started#behind-a-remote-browser-isolation-service)) (VZ-18, ADR-0202).

## From `claude/run17gh-widening` (#102, 2026-10-13)

- **ExSheet:** An italic number over `####` widens its column only to the width the same number takes upright, because ExSheet judges italic by the regular widths (ADR-0071); Excel's Font tab widened column A to 12.45 for italic, against 11.73 for a single underline or a colour, over 123456789.12 in `0.00` ([Cell Formats](exsheet/formatting#cell-formats)).

## From `claude/run17gh-glyphs` (2026-10-13)

- **ExGrid:** under a Windows display language other than English and Japanese, a number or a date can come out cut at its column's edge rather than as `####`, because `system-ui`'s widths were measured under those two only ([Columns](exgrid/columns#declaring-columns)) (FN-12e, ADR-0016).

## From `claude/run17cf-pivot-general` (2026-10-13)

- **ExPivot:** The English words are Excel's English (US) edition's, `Max of`, `Min of` and `Summarize`; the English
  (UK) edition's `Max. of`, `Min. of` and `Summarise` are not bundled, and a Consumer gives them through `Label`
  ([Values](expivot/values#aggregations); ADR-0060).
