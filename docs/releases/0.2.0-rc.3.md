## Behaviour changes

- **ExGrid:** A Window that holds a null row, a null Row Key, a Row Key twice, or without a key one object at two places, is refused by name, naming the positions; it was painted as two rows the grid could not tell apart. `GridSource.From` refuses such a list as it is made, and a replacement that is another row it holds; hand a copy, or name the rows by a key ([A source of your own](exgrid/data#a-source-of-your-own--a-million-rows); ADR-0141).
- **ExGrid:** A result whose rows fit under the browser's 33,554,428 px scroll limit but whose header band takes it past is now refused by name, as a result whose rows alone are too tall is; it was compressed. A Consumer that meets it chooses a shorter row or pages the result ([Density and row height](exgrid/appearance#density-and-row-height); ADR-0053).

## Known limitations

- **ExGrid:** under a Windows display language other than English and Japanese, a number or a date can come out cut at its column's edge rather than as `####`, because `system-ui`'s widths were measured under those two only ([Columns](exgrid/columns#declaring-columns)) (FN-12e, ADR-0016).
- **ExSheet:** `NPV` is not supported and is `#NAME?`, as `IRR`, `XIRR` and `RATE` are: its last digits differ from Excel's, and no evaluation found gives every result Excel was seen to give ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `PMT`'s payment can differ from Excel's in its last digits: 23 of the 30 payments Excel was asked differ from ExSheet's by 1 to 11 units in the last place, a relative difference of at most about 1.3e-15, which at two of them shows in the 15th significant digit; and where the evaluations Excel's payments leave part, `PMT` is `#VALUE!` ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `INDIRECT` over a Linked Table's name on its own, `INDIRECT("Positions")`, is `#VALUE!`, where Excel reads the table's data: a Linked Table is read by key, never by position ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** A Formula ExSheet refused whose array's shape could depend on a Value, such as a refused `FILTER` or `=LEN(A1:A3)`, spills nothing, and the cells its array would cover read as blank ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** An italic number over `####` widens its column only to the width the same number takes upright, because ExSheet judges italic by the regular widths (ADR-0071); Excel's Font tab widened column A to 12.45 for italic, against 11.73 for a single underline or a colour, over 123456789.12 in `0.00` ([Cell Formats](exsheet/formatting#cell-formats)).
- **ExPivot:** with no report shown — only report filters placed, or a details tab over the report — the letters typed straight after Enter on a report filter, while its Filter… opens, are dropped with every key after them rather than typed into its search; Escape typed then still closes it. With the report shown they are typed into the search ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane)) (ADR-0061, ADR-0202).

## ExGrid

- **Fixed:** Text typed, deleted or pasted into a field while a commit's answer was out is put in through the browser's own editing when it is taken, so a Ctrl+Z after it undoes it as it does the same keys typed after the answer; it was put in from script, outside the field's undo history, and a Ctrl+Z then undid nothing. ExSheet and ExPivot, drawn by ExGrid, take the same fix ([Editing](exgrid/editing#validation); ADR-0380).
- **Fixed:** Another row height or Density keeps the first row in view at the top, within its page under a pager; the grid kept the pixel offset, so it showed other rows than the ones the user was reading ([Density and row height](exgrid/appearance#density-and-row-height); ADR-0028).
- **Fixed:** A column list changed in place shows only once it is handed to the grid again; in React a scroll, and in Blazor a row fetched, could paint the changed list's cells under the headers of the list the grid had taken ([Three ways to give the grid rows](exgrid/data#three-ways-to-give-the-grid-rows); ADR-0003).

## ExPivot

- **Fixed:** A report's group rows, subtotals and grand total are set apart again, in bold, a group row on a tint and a total under a rule, and a press on a report header selects its column, as `0.1.0` did; since the report moved onto the Script Core every row was painted as a detail row and a header press did nothing ([Report forms, subtotals and grand totals](expivot/layout#report-forms--subtotals-and-grand-totals); ADR-0024).
- **Fixed:** The Blazor ExPivot paints the English (United Kingdom) edition's words again under an `en-GB` UI culture, `Max. of`, `Min. of` and `Summarise value field by`, and the Consumer's `Label` still wins; since it moved onto the Script Core it painted the United States' words under every culture ([Aggregations](expivot/values#aggregations); ADR-0060).
- **Fixed:** The expand and collapse buttons of a report's label cells are heard on the report grid's root, and a row's key is made once; each painted button carried three listeners of its own, and every pass made each painted row's key again (ADR-0350).
