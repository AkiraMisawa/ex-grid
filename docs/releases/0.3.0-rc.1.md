Packages: ExGrid, ExGrid.MudBlazor, ExSheet.Engine, ExSheet, ExSheet.MudBlazor, ExSheet.Xlsx, ExGrid.Data, ExGrid.Data.Arrow, ExGrid.Data.Xlsx, ExPivot.Engine, ExPivot, ExPivot.MudBlazor, ExChart.Engine, ExChart, ExChart.MudBlazor, ExBoard

## Highlights

- A Consumer can place a Selection of several ranges, for rows that meet a condition and do not stand together: `PlaceSelectionAsync` takes a list of ranges, and `placeSelection` in `@ex-grid/core` and on ExGrid for React's ref a range or an array of them ([Placing a selection](exgrid/selection#placing-a-selection); ADR-0050).
- A grid in a second window the page opened is told that window's Device Pixel, so its column edges and lines are sharp on a display of another scale than the opener's ([A grid in a window your page opens](getting-started/react#a-grid-in-a-window-your-page-opens); ADR-0090).
- [0.2.1](release-notes#0-2-1)'s code with what `main` took since, all of it in ExGrid. ExGrid's public surface gains one overload of `PlaceSelectionAsync`, and loses nothing.

## Known limitations

- **ExGrid:** under a Windows display language other than English and Japanese, a number or a date can come out cut at its column's edge rather than as `####`, because `system-ui`'s widths were measured under those two only ([Columns](exgrid/columns#declaring-columns)) (FN-12e, ADR-0016).
- **ExSheet:** `NPV` is not supported and is `#NAME?`, as `IRR`, `XIRR` and `RATE` are: its last digits differ from Excel's, and no evaluation found gives every result Excel was seen to give ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `PMT`'s payment can differ from Excel's in its last digits: 23 of the 30 payments Excel was asked differ from ExSheet's by 1 to 11 units in the last place, a relative difference of at most about 1.3e-15, which at two of them shows in the 15th significant digit; and where the evaluations Excel's payments leave part, `PMT` is `#VALUE!` ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `INDIRECT` over a Linked Table's name on its own, `INDIRECT("Positions")`, is `#VALUE!`, where Excel reads the table's data: a Linked Table is read by key, never by position ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** A Formula ExSheet refused whose array's shape could depend on a Value, such as a refused `FILTER` or `=LEN(A1:A3)`, spills nothing, and the cells its array would cover read as blank ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** An italic number over `####` widens its column only to the width the same number takes upright, because ExSheet judges italic by the regular widths (ADR-0071); Excel's Font tab widened column A to 12.45 for italic, against 11.73 for a single underline or a colour, over 123456789.12 in `0.00` ([Cell Formats](exsheet/formatting#cell-formats)).
- **ExPivot:** with no report shown — only report filters placed, or a details tab over the report — the letters typed straight after Enter on a report filter, while its Filter… opens, are dropped with every key after them rather than typed into its search; Escape typed then still closes it. With the report shown they are typed into the search ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane)) (ADR-0061, ADR-0202).
- **ExGrid:** the Selection Summary and ExPivot define the eleven Aggregations each in its own code, where the Definition of Done asks for one definition both answer through; one definition in `@ex-grid/data` is owed in 0.3.x ([Status-bar figures](exgrid/selection#status-bar-figures)) (DA-18, ADR-0130).
- **ExGrid:** on Blazor, an Escape that cancels a pending commit after a synchronous `OnEdit` or `OnPaste` has already written is said in the live region as cancelled, though the write stands: naming which happened needs the outcome from .NET, a change owed in 0.3.x ([Validation](exgrid/editing#validation)) (ED-34, ADR-0240).
- **ExPivot:** the Definition of Done's rows for a server's report Window (LV-24 to LV-28, LV-30, LV-31 and LV-34) judge the report Window path that ExPivot left for Leaf Aggregates, and the clauses only that path had are not met: a server's ExPivot is the Leaf Aggregate boundary's, held by its own rows, and these rows are to be rewritten for it ([A server whose data moves on](expivot/live#a-server-whose-data-moves-on)) (ADR-0370).
- **ExPivot:** Escape and Tab typed on a menu's or a panel's button while it opens, and the keys typed on a report filter's Filter… while it opens with no report shown, are not checked over the Script Core; the other keys typed on a button while its menu opens are checked ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane)) (PV-50, ADR-0061).

## ExGrid

- **Added:** A Consumer can place a Selection of several ranges, for rows that meet a condition and do not stand together. `PlaceSelectionAsync` has an overload that takes a list of ranges, and `placeSelection` in `@ex-grid/core` and on ExGrid for React's ref takes a range or an array of them. The ranges are placed as handed: in their order, ranges that overlap neither joined nor refused, with the Focus on the cell named, so the Selection read back is the one placed. A request under another Row Sequence Version is refused as the one-range form is, and no range, a range outside the grid or a Focus in none of the ranges throws. The Selection Summary's reference answer (`GridSource.From`, `GridSummary.Bands`, `summaryBands`) no longer slows with the square of the number of ranges: ten thousand one-row ranges took 2.1 seconds to their first paint in Chrome, and take 71 ms now ([Placing a selection](exgrid/selection#placing-a-selection); ADR-0050).
- **Fixed:** A grid drawn in a second window the page opened (`window.open`, through a portal, as a docking layout pops a panel out) is told that window's Device Pixel, as it is created and whenever that window's display scale or zoom changes. It was told the opener's. With the two windows on displays of different scale, the popped-out grid placed its column edges and drew its lines on the opener's device pixels, so they were blurred, and it heard nothing when its own window moved to another display. The grids ExSheet and ExPivot draw are told the same way. A grid reads its window once, as it is created: one whose element is moved into another document afterwards keeps the first window's Device Pixel, so create it again in the window it moved to (issue #565) ([A grid in a window your page opens](getting-started/react#a-grid-in-a-window-your-page-opens); ADR-0090).
