## Known limitations

- **ExGrid:** under a Windows display language other than English and Japanese, a number or a date can come out cut at its column's edge rather than as `####`, because `system-ui`'s widths were measured under those two only ([Columns](exgrid/columns#declaring-columns)) (FN-12e, ADR-0016).
- **ExSheet:** `NPV` is not supported and is `#NAME?`, as `IRR`, `XIRR` and `RATE` are: its last digits differ from Excel's, and no evaluation found gives every result Excel was seen to give ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `PMT`'s payment can differ from Excel's in its last digits: 23 of the 30 payments Excel was asked differ from ExSheet's by 1 to 11 units in the last place, a relative difference of at most about 1.3e-15, which at two of them shows in the 15th significant digit; and where the evaluations Excel's payments leave part, `PMT` is `#VALUE!` ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `INDIRECT` over a Linked Table's name on its own, `INDIRECT("Positions")`, is `#VALUE!`, where Excel reads the table's data: a Linked Table is read by key, never by position ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** A Formula ExSheet refused whose array's shape could depend on a Value, such as a refused `FILTER` or `=LEN(A1:A3)`, spills nothing, and the cells its array would cover read as blank ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** An italic number over `####` widens its column only to the width the same number takes upright, because ExSheet judges italic by the regular widths (ADR-0071); Excel's Font tab widened column A to 12.45 for italic, against 11.73 for a single underline or a colour, over 123456789.12 in `0.00` ([Cell Formats](exsheet/formatting#cell-formats)).
- **ExPivot:** with no report shown — only report filters placed, or a details tab over the report — the letters typed straight after Enter on a report filter, while its Filter… opens, are dropped with every key after them rather than typed into its search; Escape typed then still closes it. With the report shown they are typed into the search ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane)) (ADR-0061, ADR-0202).
- **ExGrid:** the Selection Summary and ExPivot define the eleven Aggregations each in its own code, where the Definition of Done asks for one definition both answer through; one definition in `@ex-grid/data` is owed in 0.2.x ([Status-bar figures](exgrid/selection#status-bar-figures)) (DA-18, ADR-0130).
- **ExGrid:** on Blazor, an Escape that cancels a pending commit after a synchronous `OnEdit` or `OnPaste` has already written is said in the live region as cancelled, though the write stands: naming which happened needs the outcome from .NET, a change owed in 0.2.x ([Validation](exgrid/editing#validation)) (ED-34, ADR-0240).
- **ExPivot:** the Definition of Done's rows for a server's report Window (LV-24 to LV-28, LV-30, LV-31 and LV-34) judge the report Window path that ExPivot left for Leaf Aggregates, and the clauses only that path had are not met: a server's ExPivot is the Leaf Aggregate boundary's, held by its own rows, and these rows are to be rewritten for it ([A server whose data moves on](expivot/live#a-server-whose-data-moves-on)) (ADR-0370).
- **ExPivot:** Escape and Tab typed on a menu's or a panel's button while it opens, and the keys typed on a report filter's Filter… while it opens with no report shown, are not checked over the Script Core; the other keys typed on a button while its menu opens are checked ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane)) (PV-50, ADR-0061).
