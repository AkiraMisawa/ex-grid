ExChart, line, step and point charts of the family's data drawn as SVG by Blazor, ships in three packages: `ExChart.Engine`, `ExChart` and `ExChart.MudBlazor`. Each series declares its Series Kind and is drawn as what it is; the chart has a tooltip, a legend that hides a series, panels, a dragged range raised as events, a summary read aloud and "View as table".
