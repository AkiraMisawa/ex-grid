A copy or a paste the report's grid refuses is now announced in its live region, in ExGrid's English or, with `PivotWords.Japanese`, in Japanese; `Label` replaces the words by the ids in ExGrid's `RefusalLabelIds`. A move made with the keys on a report taller than the window now scrolls the page to bring the cell in; a page with a fixed bar sets `scroll-padding`.
