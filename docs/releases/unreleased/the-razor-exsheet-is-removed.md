Product: ExSheet
Kind: Breaking
ADR: 0370

`ExSheet.Components.ExSheet` is drawn by the Script Core in the browser in place of .NET: the name, the document, the Workbook, the toolbar and the commands stay, so a page keeps `@using ExSheet.Components`. Its reads cross to the browser, so `ToDocument`, `CellFormatAt` and `LinkedTables` are `ToDocumentAsync`, `CellFormatAtAsync` and `LinkedTablesAsync`. `ISheetChrome` keeps the Script Core's slots (`SheetScriptSlots`, `ToolbarItem(SheetToolbarItemView)`, `FormatCells(SheetFormatCellsView)`, `SheetTab`, `SheetTabAdd`); the context overloads and their types (`ToolbarItemContext`, `ToolbarItemFace`, `ToolbarChoice`, `SheetToolbarContext`, `FormatCellsContext`, `SheetFindContext` and the Sheet Tab menu's) are removed, as are `FontToggleItem`, `ColourItem`, `AlignmentItem`, `ToolbarButton.GivesKeyboardBack` and `ToolbarItemKind.Separator`. A Toolbar Item now only declares where it stands, and `ToolbarItemBase` cannot be derived: a button of your own is a `ToolbarButton`. In ExSheet.MudBlazor, the Format Cells dialog, toolbar items, find panel and tab menu drawn for the Sheet drawn in .NET are removed; `MudSheetChrome` draws the toolbar, the Sheet Tabs and Format Cells over the Script Core, and needs no `MudDialogProvider`. ExSheet still places its stylesheet notice when the stylesheet is missing or stale (ERR-11).
