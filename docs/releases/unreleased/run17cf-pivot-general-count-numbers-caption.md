Kind: Behaviour
Docs: expivot/values#aggregations
ADR: 0060

A Count Numbers Value Field is captioned `Count of <field>`, as Excel captions it, where it read `Count Numbers of <field>`; beside a Count of the same field it takes a number, `Count of Amount2`. The bundled Japanese words are unchanged, and a Consumer that wants the old caption replaces the word `caption-count-numbers` through `Label`.
