Product: ExGrid
Kind: Added
ADR: 0440

`ExGrid.Script.ExGrid` takes the rest of the Razor component's surface: `RowKind`, `CellAppearance`, `CellChangedAt` with `ChangeHighlightDuration`, `Marks` and the Mark Column, toned columns, `CopyAnswer` and `CopyAnswerAsync`, `OnReplace`, `OnSummarize`, `ShowSelectionSummary`, `SummaryFigures` with `SummaryFiguresChanged`, `OnSelectionSummaryChanged`, `DeclaredKeys` with `OnDeclaredKey`, `PageSize`, `RangeRequestMargin`, `RowHeadingWidth` and `HideHeader`. What a row paints is answered in .NET and crosses with the row; a copy, a summary, Replace All, a declared key and a marking gesture are answered in their turn. `RowHeadings`, `NameBoxLabel`, `FindMatch` and `DataEdge` are the Consumer's own script, a `ScriptFunction`, as the Formula entry functions are (ADR-0431).
