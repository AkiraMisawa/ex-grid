`ExGrid.Components.ExGrid` takes `CommandLabel`, the grid's own words by id, as the Razor component does: its commands, its refusals, its summary, its find panel and its reload notice. The browser words the grid from the answers, asked of every id as the grid is made and whenever another function is given; the Consumer's context commands keep `ContextCommandLabel`'s words.
