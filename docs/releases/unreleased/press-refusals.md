A press the grid can no longer pair with what it was made on is refused and said: an Action's through `OnActionRefused` (`OrderMoved`, `RowLeftTheWindow` or `SourceChanged`), a Row Mark's through `OnMarkRefused` (`OrderMoved` or `SourceChanged`). With a Row Key, a press finds its row wherever the order has moved it (#64, #70).
