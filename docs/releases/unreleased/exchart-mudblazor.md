`ExChart.MudBlazor` draws the chart's toolbar and context menu with MudBlazor's controls (`MudChartChrome`), places the charts made from a grid's selection in tabs beside or below the grid (`MudChartDock`), and gives the theme's Primary, Secondary and Tertiary to the first three series.
