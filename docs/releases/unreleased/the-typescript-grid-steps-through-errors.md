`@ex-grid/core`'s `GridRuleset` takes rules over the whole collection, as the Blazor one does: `new GridRuleset(rowKey).set(rule)`, whose Breaches name their cells by Row Key, and `breaches` lists what stands for the Consumer's save to refuse. A grid given `findError` takes F8 and Shift+F8 to the next and the previous error cell anywhere in the result; `ruleset.findError(request, rowsInOrder)` answers it. An answer the order outlived moves nothing and is said, and so is a step that finds none among the rows and columns shown. F8 among `declaredKeys` beside `findError` is refused at mount.
