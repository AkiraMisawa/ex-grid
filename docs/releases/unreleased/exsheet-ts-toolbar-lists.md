ExSheet in TypeScript shows the rest of Excel's Home tab on its Sheet Toolbar, as the Blazor Sheet does. Font Colour and Fill Colour are split controls: the face sets the colour chosen last (red and yellow until one is chosen), and the arrow opens Excel's palette, whose first choice takes the colour away. Borders sets the preset chosen last, Bottom Border until one is, and its arrow lists Excel's eight presets. The Number Format menu shows the Focus cell's category and sets one with Format Cells' defaults, shows Accounting and Fraction disabled with their reasons, and opens Format Cells from More Number Formats…. A list opens in the grid's popover frame, from a press, or from ↓ or Alt+↓ on the toolbar (Enter or Space on the menu). Its arrow keys, Home and End move among the choices, Enter, Space or a press makes one, and the keyboard goes back to the grid.
