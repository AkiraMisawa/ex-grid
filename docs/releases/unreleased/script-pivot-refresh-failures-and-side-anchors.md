Kind: Fixed
ADR: 0370

Blazor's ExPivot over the Script Core (`ExPivot.Script.ExPivot`) asks its source once for a Refresh: the source's own change notice, raised while the Refresh is under way, no longer asks a second time. A question the C# source fails is shown in the source's own words, as the Razor component shows it, rather than as the bridge's "There was an exception invoking …". Both hold for a pivot in Blazor's ExBoard over the Script Core too, which crosses its C# sources the same way. And a placed field's menu stays open when the keyboard comes back to the entry's button, as a component library's menu hands it back: the entry's button, handed in by `control`, is inside its menu wherever the Chrome's anchor stands, beside the button as MudPivotChrome draws it or around it as the built-in pane does, in the TypeScript pivot and every Wrapper over it.

A button marked as a press that a Razor Chrome's contents gain as they are drawn again, a field just placed in an Area on MudPivotChrome's Field List, could be pressed in the round trip before .NET says it rendered, and the press was lost or the pivot refused it for want of the entry's anchor. Such a press now waits for the contents' wiring and is taken in its turn in the report's Input Order, ahead of every input made after it, and refused if the contents were drawn again in between, in Blazor's ExPivot and in a pivot in Blazor's ExBoard.
