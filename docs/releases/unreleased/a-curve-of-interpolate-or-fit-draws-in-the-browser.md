Product: ExChart
Kind: Added
ADR: 0420

`ExChart.Script.ExChart` draws a series made by `Interpolate.Linear`, `Interpolate.NaturalCubicSpline` or any of `Fit`'s fits: it crosses as the points or the coefficients it was made from, and the browser makes the same curve again with the Script Core's twin, held to the same cases. A series sampled from any other .NET function, a curve whose method or function was replaced, and a sampled series with a `Format` are still refused by name. `Interpolant1D` gains `Xs` and `Ys`, the points it was made from.
