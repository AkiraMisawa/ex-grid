The Headings show where the Selection is, as Excel's do: each column letter and each row number whose column or row holds part of it has a line two device pixels thick on its edge toward the cells, in the outline's green, `#217346`, as the readings of 2026-10-12 measured Excel's (R4), and in the palette's primary under ExSheet.MudBlazor. Among several ranges the Headings between them stay unmarked. `--ex-heading-mark` colours it.
