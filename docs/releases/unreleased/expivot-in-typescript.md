Product: ExPivot
Kind: Added
ADR: 0370

`@ex-grid/pivot` and `@ex-grid/pivot-react`, new npm packages, are ExPivot drawn by ExGrid in TypeScript and its React component, over `@ex-grid/pivot-engine`: the report as one grid whose rows are Windows of the report source, its labels pinned and its column Items as Header Groups, each label indented by its level, and the button beside an outer Item, or a double-click on its label, collapsing or expanding it, with Space handing that button the keyboard; a label column is as wide as its header or its widest label, which the report source estimates under the grid's text geometry; and the PivotTable Fields pane, where a field is ticked or dragged between the Areas and dropped back on the list, or on the report, to remove it. Layout ▾ on the Pivot Toolbar opens Excel's Design choices (Subtotals, Grand Totals, Report Layout), the current ones marked; each placed field's button opens its menu, with the moves, Remove Field, Sort A to Z and Z to A, and Expand and Collapse Entire Field, each disabled where it would change nothing. A menu takes the keyboard on its first enabled command, and Escape, its button or a command hands the keyboard back. A source that counts its Items has each field's count written beside it, and while a field is dragged a target in Rows or Columns the counts bound past the leaf cap is marked; a drop there is refused naming the field, its count and the cap, and the pane goes back to the report's layout; a move made while that question was out is refused with it, and each field is named in the sentence and in `onRefused`'s `fields` (ADR-0370 section 3). Moves made while a question is out are shown only as the newest question's answer. `onSelectionChanged` says the report's selection with each selected cell's row and column Items, as the Razor component's `ItemsOf` reads them, or with none where the selection reaches a row the grid does not hold drawn (ADR-0340). The words are English unless `label` gives others, such as `japanesePivotWords`, and counts are written in `locale` (the React component passes the first of the browser's languages the pivot writes in when given none, and refuses a browser with none of them, saying to pass `locale`). Filter…, Field Settings…, Value Field Settings…, the report filter band, Refresh and Show Details are not drawn yet, and a collapse does not yet keep the Focus on its Item, as the Razor component's does. They are published with 0.3.0.
