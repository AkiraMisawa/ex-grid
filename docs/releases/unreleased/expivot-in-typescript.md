Product: ExPivot
Kind: Added
ADR: 0370

`@ex-grid/pivot` and `@ex-grid/pivot-react`, new npm packages, are ExPivot drawn by ExGrid in TypeScript and its React component, over `@ex-grid/pivot-engine`: the report as one grid whose rows are Windows of the report source, its labels pinned and its column Items as Header Groups, each label indented by its level, and the button beside an outer Item, or a double-click on its label, collapsing or expanding it, with Space handing that button the keyboard; a label column is as wide as its header or its widest label, which the report source estimates under the grid's text geometry; and the PivotTable Fields pane, where a field is ticked or dragged between the Areas and dropped back on the list, or on the report, to remove it. Layout ▾ on the Pivot Toolbar opens Excel's Design choices (Subtotals, Grand Totals, Report Layout), the current ones marked; each placed field's button opens its menu, with the moves, Remove Field, Sort A to Z and Z to A, and Expand and Collapse Entire Field, each disabled where it would change nothing, and Filter… (the field's Items as the source lists them under the report's Source Version, each ticked while shown, with (Select All) and a search, which the source answers past the 10,000 Items listed; unticking every Item is refused, and Items the source cannot list are said why), Field Settings… (subtotals and order, by label or by a Value Field) or Value Field Settings… (the caption, the Aggregation, Show Values As and the number format with a sample, an Aggregation the source does not answer offered disabled with the reason, and a taken caption or an unusable format refused in the panel). A menu takes the keyboard on its first enabled command, and Escape, its button or a command hands the keyboard back. A source that counts its Items has each field's count written beside it, and while a field is dragged a target in Rows or Columns the counts bound past the leaf cap is marked; a drop there is refused naming the field, its count and the cap, and the pane goes back to the report's layout; a move made while that question was out is refused with it, and each field is named in the sentence and in `onRefused`'s `fields` (ADR-0370 section 3). Moves made while a question is out are shown only as the newest question's answer. Defer Layout Update at the pane's foot holds the pane's changes until Update, or until it is unticked, while a gesture on the report or the Pivot Toolbar applies at once and reaches the held layout too. `onSelectionChanged` says the report's selection with each selected cell's row and column Items, as the Razor component's `ItemsOf` reads them, or with none where the selection reaches a row the grid does not hold drawn (ADR-0340). The words are English unless `label` gives others, such as `japanesePivotWords`, and counts are written in `locale` (the React component passes the first of the browser's languages the pivot writes in when given none, and refuses a browser with none of them, saying to pass `locale`). Each field in Filters stands in the report filter band on the Pivot Toolbar's left, saying what it shows ((All), the one Item shown or (Multiple Items)), and its button opens Filter… over the report. A source whose features say it can be refreshed puts Refresh on the Pivot Toolbar, which asks it again under the layout on screen. A double-click on a value cell shows the records behind it (Show Details), asked of the source under the report's Source Version: `onShowDetails` hands them to a Consumer that listens, and otherwise they open in a tab at the report's foot that covers the report while selected, or in a modal dialog with `detailsView: 'dialog'`, each an ExGrid of the source's fields fetched in pages, and records the source no longer holds under that version are refused in words. The grid's Context Menu adds the report's commands after Copy, as the Razor component's does: Expand and Collapse (the Item and the entire field) on a row with Items under it, Sort A to Z and Z to A on a label, Sort Smallest to Largest and Largest to Smallest by the cell's Value Field, Show Details and Value Field Settings… on a value cell, Remove "field", and Show or Hide Field List; a command chosen once the pane no longer holds its field changes nothing. An Item expanded or collapsed, by its button, a double-click or the Context Menu, keeps the Focus in the report it brings, as in Excel. They are published with 0.3.0.
