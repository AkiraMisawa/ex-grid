ADR: 0370

`@ex-grid/pivot` and `@ex-grid/pivot-react`, new npm packages, are ExPivot drawn by ExGrid in TypeScript and its React component, over `@ex-grid/pivot-engine`: the report as one grid whose rows are Windows of the report source, its labels pinned and its column Items as Header Groups, with a double-click on an outer Item collapsing or expanding it; and the PivotTable Fields pane, where a field is ticked or dragged between the Areas and dropped back on the list, or on the report, to remove it. A source that counts its Items has each field's count written beside it, and while a field is dragged a target in Rows or Columns the counts bound past the leaf cap is marked; a drop there is refused naming the field, its count and the cap, and the pane goes back to the report's layout (ADR-0370 section 3). Moves made while a question is out are shown only as the newest question's answer. The words are English unless `label` gives others, such as `japanesePivotWords`, and counts are written in `locale` (the React component passes the browser's language when given none). The Layout menu, the field menus, Show Details and the label buttons are not drawn yet. They are published with 0.3.0.
