Product: ExSheet
Kind: Added
ADR: 0370

`ExSheet.Script.ExSheet` is Blazor's ExSheet drawn by its TypeScript Script Core, the first step of moving Blazor's ExSheet onto it (ADR-0370 section 1): the Workbook, its Formulas, the Formula Bar, the Sheet Tabs and the Selection Summary run in the browser on both hosting models, under the Razor component's parameter names and defaults. Documents cross as the JSON both engines read and write: a `Document` or `Workbook` of the Consumer's goes to the browser, every change comes back through `DocumentChanged` and `WorkbookChanged`, and one the Sheet raised, bound back two ways, crosses no more. It carries the display parameters, `SheetsChanged`, `ShowToolbarChanged`, the Selection Summary's figures, `SelectionChanged`, `AllowCopyWithHeaders`, `EditingChanged` and `IsEditing`, `OnFormatKeyRefused`, `Seed`, `Today`, `TimeZone` and `CommandLabel`, and `UndoAsync`, `RedoAsync`, `OpenFormatCellsAsync`, `ToDocumentAsync` and `ToWorkbookDocumentAsync`; a `Chrome`, `ToolbarContent`, a `PointingScope`, `OnLinkedColumnColoursChanged`, `OnSelectionSummaryChanged` and the commands that edit, format, open and save, or declare and push a Linked Table, are not carried yet, and `ExSheet.Components.ExSheet` still does all of them. Building ExSheet from source now needs Node, as ExGrid's and ExPivot's builds do, which bundles the script it ships.
