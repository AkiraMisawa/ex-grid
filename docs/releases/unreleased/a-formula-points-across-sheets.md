While a Formula is typed, a press on a Sheet's tab or Ctrl+PageUp and Ctrl+PageDown show that Sheet with the edit kept open; pointing there writes the Reference with the Sheet's name, `=Summary!B1`, and Enter commits the Formula on the Sheet it was typed on and shows that Sheet again. While a constant is typed, another Sheet is still not shown.
