`@ex-grid/sheet-mantine` is ExSheet for React's Mantine Wrapper: `MantineExSheet` stands the Sheet in Mantine's Paper and draws the Sheet Toolbar, Format Cells, the Sheet Tabs and every part of the grid inside in Mantine's components, its Chrome's colours following the theme and its scheme while the cells keep the Workbook's own font, Paper and Ink. `mantineSheetChrome` is the Wrapper's parts alone. It is published with 0.3.0.
