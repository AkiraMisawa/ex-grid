A move made with the keys, and the Cell Editor opening, now bring the cell into the window at the nearest edge, so a Sheet taller than the window scrolls the page with the Focus; a page with a fixed bar sets `scroll-padding` on its scrolling element, or a cell may be brought in under the bar.
