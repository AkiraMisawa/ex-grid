Product: ExPivot
Kind: Fixed
ADR: 0362

A Field List entry pressed straight after another input of the pivot, such as a Layout ▾ choice still being answered, now takes its turn behind that input. The choice is applied first, the entry is ticked after it, and the entry then takes the keyboard. A key typed straight after a press on a toolbar button goes where that press leaves the keyboard, not to the field ticked before it. Each of these ends as it does when done slowly.
