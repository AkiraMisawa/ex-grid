Product: ExSheet
Kind: Behaviour
ADR: 0331

Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y, and `UndoAsync` and `RedoAsync`, now show the Sheet the step was taken on and select the range it changed, as Excel's undo does: the cell typed in (not the one Enter moved to), the range pasted, filled or formatted, the whole rows or columns inserted or deleted, with the Focus on its top-left cell scrolled into view. The Selection therefore moves on an undo or a redo, also in a Workbook of one Sheet; since the grid places one range, an operation over several selects the range they make together when they make one rectangle, and otherwise the first of them in Selection order, the one it acted from (a resize of B and D alone selects B). Undoing a Sheet operation shows the Sheet it brings back or the one it leaves: a Sheet deleted, renamed or moved is shown, and when a Sheet added or copied is taken away, the Sheet it was added beside or copied from is shown (until now, the Sheet in its place); a Sheet deleted again by a redo leaves the Sheet shown, unless it is the one deleted, when the Sheet in its place is shown. The engine's `WorkbookStep.Ranges` names the ranges a step changed.
