`@ex-grid/core`'s Name Box now follows the C# core's. While a pointing outline stands over an open edit, it names the outline's Focus; while what Point wrote came from outside the grid, it is empty; while a selecting drag covers more than one cell, it shows the new `nameBoxSizeLabel` option's words for the range's size. The handle's `placeMatch` lands a Find match the Consumer found elsewhere as a step's own answer does, with the find panel left open, and `findOutcomeText`, `findPanelLabelIds` and their types are exported for a find panel slot of the Consumer's.
