ExGrid for React's `chrome` prop takes `findPanel`: the find panel and its Replace can be drawn by a design system of your own, from `useFindPanel(context)`, while the search, its answer, the Focus it moves and the Replace stay the grid's. Enter and Shift+Enter in a field step through the grid's own form. The Script Core takes the same slot as a mount function. It is published with 0.3.0.
