Product: ExChart
Kind: Added
ADR: 0370

`ExChart.Script.ExChart` and `ExChart.Script.ExSurface` are Blazor's ExChart and ExSurface drawn by their TypeScript Script Core, the first step of moving Blazor's ExChart onto it (ADR-0370 section 1): the drawing, the tooltip, the legend, Chart Filters, View as table and the turning of a surface run in the browser on both hosting models, while the `ChartDefinition` and `SurfaceDefinition` are built in .NET. They carry every parameter and event of the Razor components but `Chrome`, reading a `Spec` through its `Sources` in .NET, and `DownloadAsync` and `SvgTextAsync`. A `Format` crosses as the text it writes each value the definition holds; an axis `Format` and a `Format` on candles (give a `FormatCode` in their place), a series sampled from a .NET function of the Consumer's own (a curve made by `Interpolate` or `Fit` crosses as what it was made from, and the browser makes it again) or carrying a `Format`, a `Format` on a surface axis placed by value or on a mesh that does not write plainly and a `ZFormat` on a mesh (give a `FormatCode` or `ZFormatCode` in their place), and two Row Keys or two surface axis values that reach the browser as one value are refused by name with a `NotSupportedException`. A candle's values are written by its `FormatCode`, or else as numbers, and so is every value in its CSV (`3.3`, not a decimal's `3.30`). Building ExChart from source now needs Node, as ExGrid's and ExPivot's builds do, which bundles the script it ships.
