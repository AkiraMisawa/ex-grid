`ExGrid.InOrderAsync(handling)` runs work of your own that no control numbered, such as a command from a button outside the grid, at its place in the Input Order: after every input received so far, with the inputs after it waiting until it ends, and never taking a number told for a control's event. `ExGrid.CancellableInOrderAsync(handling, cancelledTooLate)` does the same for work that may take a while, such as reading a file: from the call until it ends the Settled Stamp names it (`data-ex-work-placed`) and the Settled Mark is off, and once its turn starts the root carries `aria-busy` and `data-ex-work` and is painted with the `--ex-pasting-*` tokens. A plain Escape typed in the grid against a page whose stamp named the work cancels its token, whether the work still waits for its turn or runs, and does nothing else; one typed against a page that did not yet name it is an ordinary key, decided by what it was typed against and not by when it arrives; an Escape that came after the work landed, or found it over otherwise, runs `cancelledTooLate` in its own turn with whether it had landed. Both may be called from any thread; disposing of the grid cancels the work under way, and one cancellable work inside another is refused. While such work waits or runs, the Settled Stamp no longer says that an Escape leaves the grid.
