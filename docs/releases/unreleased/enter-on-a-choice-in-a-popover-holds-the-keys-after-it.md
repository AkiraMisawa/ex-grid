Enter on a radio button or a checkbox in a popover's form whose OK closes the popover now holds the keys typed after it until the submission has been answered, as Enter in a text field there already did: over Blazor Server, ↓ typed straight after Enter on Format Cells' Percentage changed the choice instead of moving the Focus once Format Cells had closed. The keys are held only once the form has actually been submitted: where the browser submits nothing on such an Enter, or a handler prevents its default, the keys after it go on as typed.
