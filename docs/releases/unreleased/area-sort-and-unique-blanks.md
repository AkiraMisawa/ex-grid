Product: ExSheet
Kind: Behaviour
Docs: exsheet/formulas#supported-functions
ADR: 0125

A blank among `SORT`'s and `SORTBY`'s keys sorts after every other key, in either order, and in `UNIQUE`'s array is one of a kind only with another blank, never with 0, and spills as 0, where all three used to be `#VALUE!`: what Excel was seen to do, `SORTBY`'s descending sort and spilled blank excepted, which are answered as `SORT`'s. Read inside a Formula, a blank the three keep is counted by `COUNTA` (`=COUNTA(UNIQUE(A1:A3))` counts a blank among the values as one) and taken out by `INDEX` as a blank: Excel was seen to count `SORT`'s and `UNIQUE`'s with `COUNTA`, to find the blank `INDEX` took out of `SORT`'s blank to `ISBLANK`, and the one it took out of `UNIQUE`'s equal to `""`. Most other functions that take one Value, such as `LEN` or `MATCH`'s lookup value, read it as a blank cell, as only `ISBLANK` was seen to, of `SORT`'s, and the rest, such as `XLOOKUP`'s lookup value or `ROUND`, make the Formula `#VALUE!`; `SUMPRODUCT` and `CONCAT` take it, and `SUM` where both readings give the one sum, because every reading of it gives them the same answer; and a function whose readings of it part, such as `AVERAGE`, `MIN` or `COUNT`, makes the Formula `#VALUE!`, which `IFERROR` does not catch, a blank in a column they do not sort by included, which such a Formula used to read as a blank cell.
