Product: ExSheet
Kind: Behaviour
Docs: exsheet/formulas#supported-functions
ADR: 0125

A blank among `SORT`'s and `SORTBY`'s keys sorts after numbers, text and booleans, in either order, and in `UNIQUE`'s array is one of a kind only with another blank, never with 0, and spills as 0, where all three used to be `#VALUE!`. Excel was seen to sort a blank `SORT` key after numbers and text in either order and after a boolean ascending, and a blank `SORTBY` key after a number ascending, to spill a blank as 0, and to keep `UNIQUE`'s one of a kind only with another blank; the rest, a blank key after a boolean in a descending sort and `SORTBY`'s other keys and spilled blank, is answered as `SORT`'s by generalisation, which Excel has not yet been asked. Beside an Error Value key, a blank key makes the Formula `#VALUE!`. Read inside a Formula, a blank the three keep is counted by `COUNTA` (`=COUNTA(UNIQUE(A1:A3))` counts a blank among the values as one) and taken out by `INDEX` as a blank: Excel was seen to count `SORT`'s and `UNIQUE`'s with `COUNTA`, to find the blank `INDEX` took out of `SORT`'s blank to `ISBLANK`, and the one it took out of `UNIQUE`'s equal to `""`. Most other functions that take one Value, such as `LEN` or `MATCH`'s lookup value, read it as a blank cell, as only `ISBLANK` was seen to, of `SORT`'s, and the rest, such as `XLOOKUP`'s lookup value or `ROUND`, make the Formula `#VALUE!`; `SUMPRODUCT` and `CONCAT` take it, and `SUM` where both readings give the one sum, because every reading of it gives them the same answer; and a function whose readings of it part, such as `AVERAGE`, `MIN` or `COUNT`, makes the Formula `#VALUE!`, which `IFERROR` does not catch, a blank in a column they do not sort by included, which such a Formula used to read as a blank cell.
