## Highlights

- 0.2.0-rc.3's code with what `main` took since: a cell's Fill split diagonally between two colours (ADR-0490); two input-order fixes in the Blazor ExGrid found by the Hunt's seed sweep (ADR-0240); the marked Heading's letters readable under the Wrappers; ExChart's legend and open table keeping the keyboard; and one ExPivot fix found while the Definition of Done was measured. ExGrid's public surface added since 0.2.0-beta.1 (Find All, a Reference Outline's drag, the Focus's column band and the Split Fill) moves into its shipped baseline, the step the cuts of rc.1 to rc.3 left out. It is the candidate the Definition of Done is to be signed off against. What the version holds is in the notes of [0.2.0-rc.3](release-notes#0-2-0-rc-3), [0.2.0-rc.2](release-notes#0-2-0-rc-2), [0.2.0-rc.1](release-notes#0-2-0-rc-1), [0.2.0-beta.1](release-notes#0-2-0-beta-1) and [0.2.0-beta.3](release-notes#0-2-0-beta-3), and React starts at [React](getting-started/react) (ADR-0190, ADR-0201, ADR-0205).

## Breaking changes

None.

## Behaviour changes

- **ExGrid:** Under the Mantine, MUI and MudBlazor Wrappers, the marked Heading's letters take a Visual Token of their own, `--ex-heading-mark-color`, which is `--ex-heading-mark`'s colour unless set. A Consumer who set `--ex-heading-mark` to colour the letters under a Wrapper sets `--ex-heading-mark-color` as well ([Visual Tokens](exgrid/appearance#visual-tokens); ADR-0029).

## Known limitations

- **ExGrid:** under a Windows display language other than English and Japanese, a number or a date can come out cut at its column's edge rather than as `####`, because `system-ui`'s widths were measured under those two only ([Columns](exgrid/columns#declaring-columns)) (FN-12e, ADR-0016).
- **ExSheet:** `NPV` is not supported and is `#NAME?`, as `IRR`, `XIRR` and `RATE` are: its last digits differ from Excel's, and no evaluation found gives every result Excel was seen to give ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `PMT`'s payment can differ from Excel's in its last digits: 23 of the 30 payments Excel was asked differ from ExSheet's by 1 to 11 units in the last place, a relative difference of at most about 1.3e-15, which at two of them shows in the 15th significant digit; and where the evaluations Excel's payments leave part, `PMT` is `#VALUE!` ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `INDIRECT` over a Linked Table's name on its own, `INDIRECT("Positions")`, is `#VALUE!`, where Excel reads the table's data: a Linked Table is read by key, never by position ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** A Formula ExSheet refused whose array's shape could depend on a Value, such as a refused `FILTER` or `=LEN(A1:A3)`, spills nothing, and the cells its array would cover read as blank ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** An italic number over `####` widens its column only to the width the same number takes upright, because ExSheet judges italic by the regular widths (ADR-0071); Excel's Font tab widened column A to 12.45 for italic, against 11.73 for a single underline or a colour, over 123456789.12 in `0.00` ([Cell Formats](exsheet/formatting#cell-formats)).
- **ExPivot:** with no report shown — only report filters placed, or a details tab over the report — the letters typed straight after Enter on a report filter, while its Filter… opens, are dropped with every key after them rather than typed into its search; Escape typed then still closes it. With the report shown they are typed into the search ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane)) (ADR-0061, ADR-0202).

## ExGrid

- **Added:** A cell's appearance may split its Fill diagonally between two colours: `fill` paints the half above and left of the diagonal from the top-right corner to the bottom-left one, and the new `lowerRightFill` (`CellAppearance.LowerRightFill` in C#) paints the half below and right of it. `lowerRightFill` without `fill` is refused by name ([Font, Fill and borders per cell](exgrid/appearance#font--fill-and-borders-per-cell); ADR-0490).
- **Fixed:** On Blazor, a key typed straight after Delete, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y, a row Action or a Name Box entry waits until .NET has carried it out and its rows have reached the page. It was taken at once, against the rows from before: an F2 typed after Ctrl+Z opened the editor on the value the undo was taking back, and its commit wrote that value again ([Driving the grid from a script](exgrid/selection#driving-the-grid-from-a-script); ADR-0240).
- **Fixed:** A press made while an edit's commit waits for its verdict or its answer lands on the cell it was made on once the commit is answered. It could land rows below that cell, out of view, because where it was made was read only once the editor had closed, and a press on the editor itself could land on the grid's first cell. ExSheet and ExPivot, which ExGrid draws, are fixed with it ([Driving the grid from a script](exgrid/selection#driving-the-grid-from-a-script); ADR-0240).
- **Fixed:** The marked Heading's letters read at 4.5:1 under the Mantine, MUI and MudBlazor Wrappers, whatever the theme's primary: they drew in the Focus outline's primary, a colour chosen for a line, and an amber primary read 2.05:1. The letters now take a Visual Token of their own, `--ex-heading-mark-color`, which is `--ex-heading-mark`'s colour unless set; the Wrappers map it to a shade of the primary that reads as text, and leave the line as it was. ExSheet and ExPivot under those Wrappers show the same. Under a Wrapper, a Consumer who set `--ex-heading-mark` to colour the letters sets `--ex-heading-mark-color` as well ([Visual Tokens](exgrid/appearance#visual-tokens); ADR-0029).

## ExPivot

- **Fixed:** A layout the Consumer hands in after a refused one, such as a reset to a saved layout, takes the refusal's sentence off the Pivot Toolbar, as `0.1.0` did, and so do a source handed in and other words, locale or caps, which ask the report again; since the report moved onto the Script Core the sentence stayed over the new report until the user's next move ([Caps](expivot/sources#caps); ADR-0370).

## ExChart

- **Fixed:** A legend entry hidden or shown with Enter or Space keeps the keyboard, so a second Enter reaches it and Tab goes on to the next entry: the legend is drawn again on a toggle, and the entry that held the keyboard left with the old drawing. A legend drawn again by `hiddenSeries` (`HiddenSeries` in C#) or by new data keeps the keyboard on the same series' entry, or on the one drawn in its place when that series is gone ([The legend](exchart/panels#the-legend); ADR-0271).
- **Fixed:** An open "View as table" stays where the reader left it when the chart draws again: a series toggled from the legend, new data, a narrowed range or a switched axis rewrites only the cells whose text changed, so the table keeps its scroll position and the keyboard of a reader who tabbed into it, where it went back to its first row and dropped the keyboard to the page's body. A 3D surface's table does the same ([Read aloud](exchart/export#read-aloud); ADR-0275).
