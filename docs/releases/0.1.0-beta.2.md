# 0.1.0-beta.2

Published: 2026-10-04
Packages: ExGrid, ExGrid.MudBlazor, ExSheet.Engine, ExSheet, ExSheet.MudBlazor, ExGrid.Data, ExGrid.Data.Arrow, ExPivot.Engine, ExPivot, ExPivot.MudBlazor
Target: net10.0

## Highlights

- ExSheet, a general-purpose sheet drawn by ExGrid: cells addressed A1, a formula engine of its own that answers as Excel does, a Formula Bar, Format Cells and a toolbar ([ExSheet](exsheet)).
- ExPivot, Excel's PivotTable drawn by ExGrid: the PivotTable Fields pane, Excel's report forms and totals, Show Values As, and money summed exactly ([ExPivot](expivot)).
- The Snapshot, the family's immutable data held in columns, carried as Apache Arrow ([Snapshot](data)).
- Excel's editing keys in ExGrid: Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y handed to you, Delete, Backspace, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+R, and Find on Ctrl+F ([Bulk changes, Delete and Undo](exgrid/editing#bulk-changes--delete-and-undo)).
- Row Marks: a Mark Column whose marks your application holds ([Mark, Template and Action columns](exgrid/columns#mark--template-and-action-columns)).

## Breaking changes

- **ExGrid:** The packages target `net10.0`, where 0.1.0-beta.1 targeted `net8.0` (ADR-0022), so an application on .NET 8 or .NET 9 cannot take this version: move it to .NET 10, or stay on 0.1.0-beta.1 ([Requirements](getting-started#requirements)). This was not said when the version was published (#21).
- **ExGrid:** The stylesheets are minified and renamed. Link `_content/ExGrid/ex-grid.min.css` in place of `_content/ExGrid/ex-grid.css`, and `_content/ExGrid.MudBlazor/mud-ex-grid.min.css` in place of `_content/ExGrid.MudBlazor/mud-ex-grid.css`: a link left as it was finds no stylesheet, and the grid is drawn unstyled ([Link the stylesheets](getting-started#link-the-stylesheets); ADR-0123, #57).
- **ExGrid:** `ViewportSize.Fill` is renamed `ViewportSize.Stretch`, and `IsFill` is renamed `IsStretch`, because Fill now names Excel's gesture. What it does is unchanged (ADR-0028, #25).

## Behaviour changes

- **ExGrid:** The Focus is Excel's active cell, the end that stays when a range is extended. After Shift+↓, typing enters the cell the range began at, as in Excel; 0.1.0-beta.1 entered the cell at the moving end (ADR-0052, #30).
- **ExGrid:** The grid takes keys it left to the browser. Ctrl+F opens the grid's Find, or is refused through `OnFindRefused` where nothing can search every row; on a grid with an editable column, Delete raises `OnClear` and Backspace opens an empty Cell Editor (ADR-0035, ADR-0054, ADR-0055, #25).
- **ExGrid:** Alt+↓, and a header's ▾, open one popover: the column's commands above its filter, with Excel's letters, where 0.1.0-beta.1 opened the column menu (ADR-0044, #23).
- **ExGrid:** The grid draws its own scrollbars, 12 px wide on every platform, in place of the browser's (ADR-0029, #30).
- **ExGrid:** Column edges lie on the screen's own pixels, so a column can be painted up to one of them narrower than declared (ADR-0090, #30).
- **ExGrid:** A result taller than the browser can lay out is scrolled with a compressed scroll height, so every row can be reached. 0.1.0-beta.1 refused one past 2²⁵ px, and at a display scale above 100% left its last rows out of reach (ADR-0053, #30).
- **ExGrid:** A Fixed width above `MaxWidth` is kept, where 0.1.0-beta.1 refused it: `MaxWidth` bounds only what the grid computes. A Fixed width below `MinWidth` is refused by `GridColumn`'s constructor, naming the column (ADR-0016, #18, #19).
- **ExGrid:** After an Action fires, the grid moves no focus: the keyboard stays where it was before the press, or goes where your handler takes it (ADR-0037, #24).

## ExGrid

- **Added:** The grid runs under Blazor Server as well as WebAssembly, and every browser test runs against both hosts. Support for Server is declared once the runs it still owes are in (#21).
- **Added:** Row Marks: a Mark Column whose checkboxes mark rows your application holds, with every row marked from the header (ADR-0043, #22).
- **Added:** Excel's editing keys: Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y and Ctrl+Shift+Z handed to you as `OnUndo` and `OnRedo`, Delete as a Clear Intent through `OnClear`, Backspace, and Ctrl+D and Ctrl+R filling from the range's first row or column (ADR-0007, ADR-0035, ADR-0054, #25).
- **Added:** Find: Ctrl+F asks the source, or you through `OnFind`, and moves the Focus to the next match, in a find panel any Chrome can draw (ADR-0055, #25).
- **Added:** The Change Highlight: a cell whose value just changed is marked for a moment in one colour, asked of you through `CellChangedAt` ([Live updates and the Change Highlight](exgrid/data#live-updates-and-the-change-highlight); ADR-0068, #30).
- **Added:** What ExSheet and ExPivot ask of the grid, open to any Consumer: formula entry, completion and Point mode; the Keyboard Field, so an IME can start on a selected cell; and the keyboard handed back to your application (ADR-0050, ADR-0051, ADR-0063, ADR-0070, ADR-0080, #30).
- **Added:** The filter panel's list of a column's values (ADR-0009, #23).

## ExSheet

- **Added:** ExSheet, a general-purpose sheet drawn by ExGrid as its Consumer: cells addressed A1, Entries and Formulas, a Name Box and a Formula Bar, and a Sheet Document with one undo stack (ADR-0046, ADR-0048, ADR-0051, #30).
- **Added:** `ExSheet.Engine`, a formula engine of ExSheet's own whose functions give Excel's results, checked against answers read from Excel: a function outside its declared set is `#NAME?`. It spills an array as Excel 365 does, and `TODAY` and `NOW` can follow the user's day rather than the server's ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas); ADR-0047, ADR-0121, ADR-0122, ADR-0125, #57, #58, #59).
- **Added:** Cell Formats painted on white paper, Format Cells, and the Sheet Toolbar with Excel's KeyTips ([Formatting](exsheet/formatting); ADR-0071, ADR-0100, #30).
- **Added:** Linked Tables, your data read into the sheet by key; Reference Outlines in colour while a formula is edited; and pointing at cells across grids ([Linked Tables](exsheet/linked-tables); ADR-0049, ADR-0057, ADR-0058, #30).
- **Added:** `ExSheet.MudBlazor`, MudBlazor's Chrome for the sheet and its dialogs (#30).

## ExPivot

- **Added:** ExPivot, Excel's PivotTable drawn by ExGrid: the PivotTable Fields pane, the Compact, Outline and Tabular forms, subtotals and grand totals, Show Values As and Show Details ([Layout](expivot/layout); ADR-0059, ADR-0061, #30).
- **Added:** `ExPivot.Engine`, which answers as Excel's PivotTable does, over your objects, a Snapshot or a CSV, or a server's source through `PivotSource.Fetch` ([Sources](expivot/sources); ADR-0060, ADR-0066, #30).
- **Added:** Live data: a Change Batch makes the next Snapshot, and the report folds it in with the Change Highlight ([Live data](expivot/live); ADR-0067, ADR-0068, #30).
- **Added:** `ExPivot.MudBlazor`, a Chrome and a stylesheet (ADR-0062, #30).

## Data

- **Added:** `ExGrid.Data`: the Snapshot, the family's immutable data held in columns, made from objects, from a CSV under a Schema or from columns in slices, and moved on by Change Batches (ADR-0064, ADR-0067, #30).
- **Added:** `ExGrid.Data.Arrow`: a Snapshot written and read as Apache Arrow ([Apache Arrow](data/arrow); ADR-0065, #30).
