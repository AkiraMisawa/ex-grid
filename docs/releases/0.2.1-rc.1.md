# 0.2.1-rc.1

Published: 2026-11-11
Packages: ExGrid, ExGrid.MudBlazor, ExSheet.Engine, ExSheet, ExSheet.MudBlazor, ExSheet.Xlsx, ExGrid.Data, ExGrid.Data.Arrow, ExGrid.Data.Xlsx, ExPivot.Engine, ExPivot, ExPivot.MudBlazor, ExChart.Engine, ExChart, ExChart.MudBlazor, ExBoard
Target: net10.0

## Highlights

- A compact header: `HeaderDisplay` (`headerDisplay` in ExGrid for React) gives the column menu's ▾ and the marks of the column's sort and filter one box, so a header needs 28 px less on a column that can be sorted and has the menu ([A compact header](exgrid/columns#a-compact-header); ADR-0500).
- A filtered column says so in its header: the column menu's ▾ is drawn as a funnel while the `Filter` holds a spec for its column, and a header whose column menu is hidden shows the funnel as a mark ([Sorting, filtering and find](exgrid/sorting-filtering); ADR-0501, ADR-0502).
- Under the Mantine, MUI and MudBlazor Wrappers the Focus outline, the Heading Mark's line, the grid's focus ring and the editor's underline read 3:1 whatever the theme's primary, and the Action Column's buttons and Mantine's disabled menu items take the theme ([Visual Tokens](exgrid/appearance#visual-tokens); ADR-0030).
- [0.2.0](release-notes#0-2-0)'s code with what `main` took since, all of it in ExGrid's header and under the Wrappers. ExGrid's public surface gains `HeaderDisplay`, `GridHeaderDisplay` and two overloads of `GridMetrics.HeaderRequiredPx`, and loses nothing.

## Behaviour changes

- **ExGrid:** A filtered column now says so in its header: while the `Filter` holds a spec for a column, the column menu's ▾ in that column's header is drawn as a funnel, in the default header as in the compact one, and the button is named `Column menu, filtered` for a screen reader. No column's width changes, and a click, a press on the ▾ and Alt+↓ mean what they meant. A grid that shows the column menu and is given a filter will look different on its filtered columns; a test that finds the ▾ of a filtered column by the exact name `Column menu` should find it by `Column menu, filtered` or by its class ([Sorting, filtering and find](exgrid/sorting-filtering); ADR-0501).
- **ExGrid:** With `HideColumnMenu` (`hideColumnMenu` in ExGrid for React) a filtered column's header now shows the funnel too. The default header, which has no ▾ then and keeps no room for one, takes the ▾'s band for the funnel while the `Filter` holds a spec for its column: a mark named `Filtered` for a screen reader, with nothing to press. A grid that hides the column menu and is given a filter will look different on its filtered columns, and an Auto column whose header decides its width is 22 px wider for as long as it is filtered; a width that was declared, dragged, fitted or restored is not changed, and no width is reported. The compact header already marked such a column in its box; its one header without a box, a column that cannot be sorted, takes the box the same way. `GridMetrics.HeaderRequiredPx` has an overload that takes `filtered` (`headerRequiredPx` and `gridHeaderRequiredPx` take it in `@ex-grid/core`), and charges it only for these headers ([A compact header](exgrid/columns#a-compact-header); ADR-0502).
- **ExGrid:** Changing `HideColumnMenu` (`hideColumnMenu` in ExGrid for React) on a grid in use no longer measures its Auto columns afresh. An Auto width now keeps what its values taught it and gives up, or takes back, only the room its header kept for the ▾: a column its header decided still narrows and widens by that room, and a column an earlier, wider value had grown stays at that width, where it used to come back at the width of the rows painted at that moment. Nothing changes for a grid that sets `HideColumnMenu` once ([A compact header](exgrid/columns#a-compact-header); ADR-0500).
- **ExGrid:** Under the Mantine, MUI and MudBlazor Wrappers the Focus outline, the Heading Mark's line, the grid's focus ring and the editor's underline read 3:1 against the grid's ground and the Heading Mark's shades whatever the theme's primary, in either scheme (#556). They drew in the primary as it was in the light scheme, so an amber primary's Focus read 2.7:1 on white and its line 2.0:1 on the shade, and a saturated primary's line fell short on the dark shade. The primary is now darkened in a light scheme and lightened, its chroma softened, in a dark one, hue kept, only as far as 3:1 needs: Mantine's default blue draws a shade darker in the light scheme. Under MudBlazor's dark scheme a marked Heading's shade is now a little darker than the surface rather than lighter, and a whole column's or row's is the primary's tint over that darker ground, so the line clears both as well as an ExSheet's white Paper. ExSheet's and ExPivot's grids under those Wrappers show the same; ExSheet's own toolbar, swatch and tab marks under MUI and MudBlazor still take the primary as it is ([Visual Tokens](exgrid/appearance#visual-tokens); ADR-0030).

## Known limitations

- **ExGrid:** under a Windows display language other than English and Japanese, a number or a date can come out cut at its column's edge rather than as `####`, because `system-ui`'s widths were measured under those two only ([Columns](exgrid/columns#declaring-columns)) (FN-12e, ADR-0016).
- **ExSheet:** `NPV` is not supported and is `#NAME?`, as `IRR`, `XIRR` and `RATE` are: its last digits differ from Excel's, and no evaluation found gives every result Excel was seen to give ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `PMT`'s payment can differ from Excel's in its last digits: 23 of the 30 payments Excel was asked differ from ExSheet's by 1 to 11 units in the last place, a relative difference of at most about 1.3e-15, which at two of them shows in the 15th significant digit; and where the evaluations Excel's payments leave part, `PMT` is `#VALUE!` ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `INDIRECT` over a Linked Table's name on its own, `INDIRECT("Positions")`, is `#VALUE!`, where Excel reads the table's data: a Linked Table is read by key, never by position ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** A Formula ExSheet refused whose array's shape could depend on a Value, such as a refused `FILTER` or `=LEN(A1:A3)`, spills nothing, and the cells its array would cover read as blank ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** An italic number over `####` widens its column only to the width the same number takes upright, because ExSheet judges italic by the regular widths (ADR-0071); Excel's Font tab widened column A to 12.45 for italic, against 11.73 for a single underline or a colour, over 123456789.12 in `0.00` ([Cell Formats](exsheet/formatting#cell-formats)).
- **ExPivot:** with no report shown — only report filters placed, or a details tab over the report — the letters typed straight after Enter on a report filter, while its Filter… opens, are dropped with every key after them rather than typed into its search; Escape typed then still closes it. With the report shown they are typed into the search ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane)) (ADR-0061, ADR-0202).
- **ExGrid:** the Selection Summary and ExPivot define the eleven Aggregations each in its own code, where the Definition of Done asks for one definition both answer through; one definition in `@ex-grid/data` is owed in 0.2.x ([Status-bar figures](exgrid/selection#status-bar-figures)) (DA-18, ADR-0130).
- **ExGrid:** on Blazor, an Escape that cancels a pending commit after a synchronous `OnEdit` or `OnPaste` has already written is said in the live region as cancelled, though the write stands: naming which happened needs the outcome from .NET, a change owed in 0.2.x ([Validation](exgrid/editing#validation)) (ED-34, ADR-0240).
- **ExPivot:** the Definition of Done's rows for a server's report Window (LV-24 to LV-28, LV-30, LV-31 and LV-34) judge the report Window path that ExPivot left for Leaf Aggregates, and the clauses only that path had are not met: a server's ExPivot is the Leaf Aggregate boundary's, held by its own rows, and these rows are to be rewritten for it ([A server whose data moves on](expivot/live#a-server-whose-data-moves-on)) (ADR-0370).
- **ExPivot:** Escape and Tab typed on a menu's or a panel's button while it opens, and the keys typed on a report filter's Filter… while it opens with no report shown, are not checked over the Script Core; the other keys typed on a button while its menu opens are checked ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane)) (PV-50, ADR-0061).

## ExGrid

- **Added:** `HeaderDisplay` (`headerDisplay` in ExGrid for React) chooses how every header of a grid lays out what stands beside its label (ADR-0500). `Standard`, the default, is the layout as it was. `Compact` gives the column menu's ▾ and the marks of the column's sort and filter one box inside the cell's padding, so a header needs 28 px less on a column that can be sorted and has the menu; the box shows an arrow, a funnel or both while the column holds a sort or a filter, and a press on it opens the column menu. An Auto width counts the header of the display chosen, and a change of display replaces only that part of it; a width that was declared, dragged, fitted or restored is never changed by it ([A compact header](exgrid/columns#a-compact-header)).
- **Fixed:** Under the Mantine, MUI and MudBlazor Wrappers the Action Column's buttons take the theme's colours in either scheme (#553): Mantine's default Button, MUI's paper and text, MudBlazor's outlined default. They kept the browser's own grey button, which in a dark scheme was the brightest thing in the row. Their box is unchanged, so an Auto column keeps its width ([Mark, Template and Action columns](exgrid/columns#mark--template-and-action-columns)).
- **Fixed:** Under `@ex-grid/mantine` a disabled command in a column menu or the Context Menu is drawn as Mantine's Menu draws a disabled item, its label dimmed on no shade of its own (#552). Mantine's Button filled every disabled one grey, and a run of them read as one disabled panel ([Visual Tokens](exgrid/appearance#visual-tokens)).
