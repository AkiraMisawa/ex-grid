# 0.2.0-rc.1

Published: pending
Packages: ExGrid, ExGrid.MudBlazor, ExSheet.Engine, ExSheet, ExSheet.MudBlazor, ExSheet.Xlsx, ExGrid.Data, ExGrid.Data.Arrow, ExGrid.Data.Xlsx, ExPivot.Engine, ExPivot, ExPivot.MudBlazor, ExChart.Engine, ExChart, ExChart.MudBlazor, ExBoard
Target: net10.0

## Highlights

- ExChart, a new product in three packages: line, step and point charts, Observations and 3D surfaces of the family's data, drawn as SVG by Blazor and saved as CSV, SVG and PNG; a chart made from a grid's selection follows its rows, and an AI draws one by writing a Chart Spec that never holds a number ([Overview](exchart); ADR-0250, ADR-0320).
- ExBoard, a new package: a Board of ExGrid, ExPivot and ExChart views over typed inputs, JSON an AI composes once and a workflow runs without AI, held on a server where an input is too large to download ([Overview](exboard); ADR-0340).
- ExSheet holds a Workbook: several Sheets in tabs, a Formula that reads another Sheet or a 3D Reference across them, and Sheets added, renamed, moved, copied and deleted as Excel does ([Workbook](exsheet/workbook); ADR-0331).
- `.xlsx` files, opened into ExSheet and saved from it with `ExSheet.Xlsx`, and read and written on a server with no component by `ExGrid.Data.Xlsx` ([Open and save .xlsx](exsheet/xlsx); ADR-0330).
- Find and Replace: Ctrl+H in ExGrid and ExSheet, across a Workbook too, and Ctrl+F in ExPivot's report ([Replace](exgrid/sorting-filtering#replace); ADR-0332).

## Breaking changes

- **ExGrid:** A tick of the find panel's Match case or Match entire cell contents is now a click taken in its place in the Input Order, under the built-in Chrome and `MudGridChrome` alike: a step clicked straight after a tick searches with the option ticked, and one clicked before it with the option as it stood, at machine speed and over a round trip. `FindContext.MatchCaseChanged` and `WholeCellChanged` are now `Func<bool, Task>` where they were `Action<bool>`, so a Chrome of your own that draws the find panel returns the task from its option's change handler (`ValueChanged="Context.MatchCaseChanged"` binds as it did), and puts `data-ex-claims="click"` on the checkbox itself, not on a label around it (ADR-0240).
- **ExSheet:** `ExSheet.Engine` is rebuilt around `Workbook`, with no forwarders kept: `new Sheet(culture)` is now `new Workbook(culture)`, whose one Sheet is `Sheet1`, and `new Sheet(culture, name)` is `new Workbook(culture, [name])`; the Sheet is `workbook.Sheets[0]`, read through `GetValue`, `GetDisplay`, `GetCellFormat` and their peers (ADR-0331).
- **ExSheet:** `Sheet.Open(document)` is now `Workbook.Open(document)`, which opens a Sheet Document as a Workbook of that one Sheet, and `sheet.ToDocument()` is `workbook.ToDocument(sheet.Name)` (ADR-0331).
- **ExSheet:** `sheet.Do(edit)` and `sheet.Check(edit)` are now `workbook.Do(sheet.Name, edit)`, which returns a `WorkbookStep`, and `workbook.Check(sheet.Name, edit)`; `sheet.Enter`, `SetEntries`, `InsertRows`, `DeleteRows`, `InsertColumns`, `DeleteColumns`, `SetNumberFormat`, `SetAlignment`, `SetCellFormat`, `SetColumnWidth` and `SetAutomaticColumnWidth` are `workbook.Do(sheet.Name, …)` with the `SheetEdit` of the same name, and what the edit changed on the Sheet is `step.Change.For(sheet)` (ADR-0331).
- **ExSheet:** `sheet.DeclareLinkedTable`, `sheet.PushLinkedTable` and `sheet.LinkedTables` are now the `Workbook`'s members of the same names, as the tables are read from every Sheet (ADR-0331).
- **ExSheet:** `sheet.Today`, `SetToday`, `NowSource`, `RecalculateVolatile` and `HasVolatileFormulas` are now the `Workbook`'s members of the same names, which answer for every Sheet, so a Sheet added or copied reads the Sheet Day too, where it showed `#GETTING_DATA` before (ADR-0331).
- **ExSheet:** `sheet.Seed`, `Changes` and `SetSeed` are now the `Workbook`'s members of the same names, and `SetSeed` answers a `WorkbookChange`. A change on any Sheet now draws `RAND` and `RANDBETWEEN` again on every Sheet, as Excel does, and a draw names its Sheet, so the same seed and count give different draws than before and `=RAND()` at the same cell of two Sheets draws two numbers (ADR-0180).
- **ExSheet:** `FormatCellsContext` no longer has `ReturnKeyboardFrom` or `HoldKeysForPopup`: a Chrome's own Format Cells no longer holds the keys typed on the way itself. It uses `InOrder` for a claimed control's handler, `JoinSurface` for a dialog or popup drawn outside the Sheet, `HandKeyboardToFrame` and `TakeKeyboard` to bring the keyboard to it, and `ReturnKeyboard` to give it back; the Sheet holds the keys meanwhile. Under ExSheet.MudBlazor, the Format Cells dialog and its More Colours popup are in the Sheet's order — Escape, OK, Cancel, × and More Colours' opener are handled in their place, and what is typed after them goes where they leave the keyboard — and `MudFormatCellsDialog` loses its `KeysArrive` and `KeyWhileClosing` parameters, which only the Wrapper set ([Format Cells](exsheet/formatting#format-cells); ADR-0240).
- **ExSheet:** `ToolbarItemContext` no longer has `ChoicesClosing` or `ChoicesOpened`, and `ChoicesClosed` now takes the choice made and whether Escape closed the list: a Chrome's own toolbar list no longer passes the keys typed on the way to the toolbar or answers the keys it hands back. It calls `ChoicesClosed` in the turn of the key or press that closes it, while it still holds the keyboard, and the toolbar makes the choice itself; it takes the keyboard through the new `TakeKeyboard`, joins the Sheet's order through `JoinSurface` when it is drawn outside the Sheet, and puts its keys and presses in their turn through `InOrder`. A list asked to open that cannot calls `ChoicesClosed` with no choice. Under ExSheet.MudBlazor the toolbar's buttons and lists are in the Sheet's order as the built-in ones are: a press on an item, and a key or a choice in its list, is answered in its place, and what is typed after it goes where it leaves the keyboard ([The Sheet Toolbar](exsheet/formatting#the-sheet-toolbar); ADR-0240).
- **ExPivot:** `PivotPopupFrame.OnFocusIn` and `OnFocusOut` are removed. The frame is ExPivot's own part, placed by ExPivot and hidden from a Consumer's editor; they carried the local queue of keys typed while a menu or a panel opened, which the pivot's Input Order replaces. Nothing replaces them (ADR-0240).

## Behaviour changes

- **ExGrid:** An `OperationCanceledException` thrown by `OnFind` (or a Source's find) for a step the grid did not cancel is now taken as a step the Consumer gave up: the Focus does not move, the find panel stays open with no outcome, and nothing is raised on `OnFindRefused`. Before, the exception escaped the grid. A Consumer that cannot read what it searches throws it and says why itself, rather than answering `NotFound` for cells it never read (ADR-0055).
- **ExGrid:** A click on one of the grid's own controls — a heading, a column's ▾, a column menu or Context Menu item, a completion candidate, the pager, the status line's buttons, a grip's double click, a filter panel's OK and Cancel, Find's Previous and Next, the context menu on the rows and on the status line, under the built-in Chrome and the MudBlazor Wrapper alike — now takes its place in the grid's Input Order: it is handled after every key, press or click made before it and before every one made after it, on Blazor Server and WebAssembly alike, and one made while earlier keys are still being answered is held and carried out in its turn (ADR-0240). The Settled Mark waits for it. Nothing changes for a script that waited for each effect; one that sent such clicks at machine speed now gets the outcome it gets sending them slowly ([Selection and keyboard](exgrid/selection); ADR-0240).
- **ExGrid:** Find now searches the visible column headers and Header Group labels as well as the rows, the header rows coming before the first data row: a step that wraps past the last row, or finds no row, stops on the first header that matches (the last, backward), and the next step goes on from it. A header found is the current match: it wears `ex-match-mark-current`, is scrolled into view across, and the panel names it, "Found in the header Trader" (`FindOutcome.FoundInHeader`, `FindPanelLabelIds.FoundInHeader`, the header's text in the new `FindContext.FoundHeader`), while the header carries `aria-current="true"`; the Focus does not move. Every header on screen that matches wears `ex-match-mark`, judged by `GridFind.Matches` over its text whoever answers Find. A search for text that is also a column's header therefore stops on that header once per round. The Consumer's `GridFindRequest` and answer are unchanged. A selection of more than one cell searches no header, hidden columns' headers are not searched, Replace never writes a header, and the new `FindsHeaders` parameter (default `true`) turns it off, as ExSheet does for its column letters (ADR-0333).
- **ExGrid:** Keys held while the grid answers an earlier key or click no longer give up after two seconds: they wait for the editor, a popover or your frame to take the keyboard, however long the round trip, and a reveal's scroll, an edit's close and a field's text no longer stop waiting on a clock either. When the grid has answered everything before them and owes the keyboard to nothing, keys whose keyboard never arrived are refused, every one, and the live region says so in the words of the new `KeyRefusalLabelIds.KeyboardDidNotArrive`, which a Consumer replaces or silences through `CommandLabel` (ADR-0240). Before, they were replayed against whatever held the keyboard, or dropped silently. The keys a popover answers itself — Enter, Space, a letter or Tab on a menu item, a letter on a filter's value list, a filter or Find submitted with Enter, Tab wrapping round a popover — are now numbered too, under the built-in Chrome and the MudBlazor Wrapper alike, so nothing is left waiting on a clock. Breaking for a substituted Chrome: a menu must resolve its keys through the context's `ResolveKey` and run, close and focus through the context's delegates in the same handler, and a control or form that should keep its place says so with `data-ex-claims` and then reaches the grid every time that event comes — a filter form hands each submission to `Apply`, one that cannot be applied as the answer `Apply` refuses; a Chrome that marks nothing still works, unnumbered. The MudBlazor Wrapper's filter panel now hands a submission it cannot apply to `Apply` that way rather than dropping it ([Selection and keyboard](exgrid/selection); ADR-0240).
- **ExGrid:** Row Action and row Mark clicks now wait for earlier input, including asynchronous Consumer callbacks, and recovery of a discarded Blazor click uses the same input number. While waiting, a command follows its row only while continuously present in the Window; leaving it causes a visible refusal even if the row returns, and Consumers handling `OnMarkRefused` should handle the new `RowLeftTheWindow` reason (Source replacement still reports `SourceChanged`) ([Mark, Template and Action columns](exgrid/columns#mark--template-and-action-columns); ADR-0240, ADR-0160, ADR-0142, ADR-0043).
- **ExSheet:** A Formula with functions nested more than 64 levels deep, Excel's limit, or nested more than 256 levels deep (parentheses, signs, functions, operations within operations) is now refused as it is typed, with the reason; so is such text typed after a sign without `=`, which stays a refused Formula rather than becoming text. A Consumer will see a Sheet Document or an `.xlsx` workbook holding such a Formula refused on open, whole, where it opened before (ADR-0047).
- **ExSheet:** A Sheet now draws its find panel through its own Chrome, so a Consumer Chrome's `IGridChrome.FindPanel` is no longer asked on a Sheet: ExSheet wraps the Chrome it is handed and draws the Sheet's panel, with Replace and Within, in its place. A Chrome of your own that drew the grid's find panel on a Sheet implements `ISheetChrome.SheetFindPanel` instead, handed a `SheetFindContext`; one that does not gets ExSheet's built-in Sheet panel (ADR-0332).
- **ExSheet:** A structured reference is stored in its Linked Table's declared case, the table's name and the column's, as Excel writes one back: `=sum(limit[line])` over `LIMIT` with a column `Line` is stored, shown and saved as `=SUM(LIMIT[Line])`. A Formula typed before its table is declared keeps the case it was typed in (ADR-0049).
- **ExSheet:** In a Workbook of several Sheets, a command names the Sheet it acts on: `DoAsync(sheet, edit)` does an edit, a Sheet operation among them, on that Sheet whether it is shown or not, and `CellFormatAt(sheet, address)` reads a cell there. `DoAsync(edit)` and `CellFormatAt(address)`, which name none, are refused with `SheetRefusalReason.SheetNotNamed` once the Workbook holds several Sheets, and work as before while it holds one; a page that adds Sheets from code names the Sheet. The new `SheetsChanged` event hears every Sheet added, deleted, renamed, moved or copied, an undo or redo of one included, with the names before and after (ADR-0331).
- **ExSheet:** ExSheet shows a Workbook: bind `Workbook` (a `WorkbookDocument`) in place of `Document`, and a tab for each Sheet stands under the grid, switched by a press or by Ctrl+PageUp and Ctrl+PageDown, each Sheet keeping its own Selection and scroll position. `WorkbookChanged` and `ToWorkbookDocument()` carry the Workbook; `DocumentChanged` is raised only while it holds one Sheet, and `ToDocument()` throws once it holds several. The Sheet Tabs show by default, also under a single Sheet, in a strip as tall as the Formula Bar taken from the height you give ExSheet, so the grid of every existing ExSheet is one row shorter; `ShowSheetTabs="false"` gives the height back. A write or typing aimed at the Sheet left that reaches the component after the switch is refused, and its notice now says "another Sheet was shown, or another Sheet Document opened". `Sheet.Workbook` is public (ADR-0331).
- **ExSheet:** A new Sheet is numbered as Excel numbers one: `Sheet` and the next number of a count of the Sheets the Workbook has made since it was created or opened, copies included, passing over a name a Sheet has. With Sheet1 renamed Sheet5, the next Sheet is Sheet2, where it was Sheet6. Undoing an insertion gives its number back, and a Workbook opened from a document starts from the first number no Sheet has (ADR-0331).
- **ExSheet:** The Sheet's own surfaces now take their place in its Input Order, on Blazor Server and WebAssembly alike: a press on a Sheet Toolbar item or a key on the toolbar, a press on a Sheet Tab or a key on the tab strip, Format Cells' OK and Cancel under the built-in Chrome, and the keyboard coming into the Sheet or leaving it (which a Pointing Scope listens to). What is typed straight after any of them is taken once it has been answered, where it left the keyboard — Bold pressed and ↓ typed at once set bold on the Focus cell and then move the Focus; a press on a tab and `77`, Enter at once land on the Sheet shown. The Sheet's outermost element carries `data-ex-input-root`. Under ExSheet.MudBlazor, the toolbar's MudBlazor lists keep the holds they had; Format Cells' dialog joins the order (see its own note) ([Formatting](exsheet/formatting); ADR-0240).
- **ExSheet:** Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y, and `UndoAsync` and `RedoAsync`, now show the Sheet the step was taken on and select the range it changed, as Excel's undo does: the cell typed in (not the one Enter moved to), the range pasted, filled or formatted, the whole rows or columns inserted or deleted, with the Focus on its top-left cell scrolled into view. The Selection therefore moves on an undo or a redo, also in a Workbook of one Sheet; since the grid places one range, an operation over several selects the range they make together when they make one rectangle, and otherwise the first of them in Selection order, the one it acted from (a resize of B and D alone selects B). Undoing a Sheet operation shows the Sheet it brings back or the one it leaves: a Sheet deleted, renamed or moved is shown, and when a Sheet added or copied is taken away, the Sheet it was added beside or copied from is shown (until now, the Sheet in its place); a Sheet deleted again by a redo leaves the Sheet shown, unless it is the one deleted, when the Sheet in its place is shown. The engine's `WorkbookStep.Ranges` names the ranges a step changed (ADR-0331).
- **ExPivot:** A press, a key or a tick in the PivotTable Fields pane, its menus and panels, the Pivot Toolbar, the report filter band, the report's expand toggles and the Details tabs and dialog now takes its place in the report's Input Order, under the built-in Chrome and the MudBlazor Wrapper alike: one made while an earlier input is still being answered waits for it, and the keys typed meanwhile go where that answer left the keyboard, on Blazor Server and WebAssembly alike (ADR-0240). Enter and Space press the pivot's buttons at their keydown. The local queues that held the keys typed while a menu or a panel opened or closed are gone; a script that sent such input at machine speed now gets the outcome it gets sending it slowly. A field dropped on the pane or off the report takes its place in the order too, under any Chrome, and is refused if an input answered before it replaced the layout it was dropped on. A Chrome of your own that does not use the context's `InOrder` keeps a closing menu or panel standing until its opener has the keyboard back, as before. Each details grid keeps an Input Order of its own ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane); ADR-0240).
- **ExPivot:** With no report grid, the pivot's inputs now take their turns one after another, in the order they arrived, instead of each being handled at once: a menu command or a panel opened while an earlier one was still handing the keyboard on no longer loses the keyboard (ADR-0360).
- **ExChart:** A 3D surface is seen from above and turns the way it is dragged. It was projected as seen from below its floor, so the picture read as a mirror image and every drag looked reversed. A drag now holds the surface where it was pressed, as Plotly's turntable does: a drag the box's height turns it a whole turn, the pitch runs from -90 to 90 so it may be seen from below, the arrow keys turn it as a drag the same way would (ArrowDown now tips its top toward the viewer, where it did the opposite), the right button pans, and the wheel zooms by how far it moved. A `SurfaceView` kept from before this change is now drawn from above at the same angles, so it no longer gives the same picture (ADR-0274).

## Known limitations

- **ExGrid:** behind a remote browser isolation service, the repaint after Ctrl+↓, Ctrl+↑ or PageDown is not checked: its browser test passes, but the check behind such a service was not run for this version, as it was not for `0.1.0` ([Behind a remote browser isolation service](getting-started#behind-a-remote-browser-isolation-service)) (VZ-18, ADR-0202).
- **ExGrid:** under a Windows display language other than English and Japanese, a number or a date can come out cut at its column's edge rather than as `####`, because `system-ui`'s widths were measured under those two only ([Columns](exgrid/columns#declaring-columns)) (FN-12e, ADR-0016).
- **ExGrid:** on macOS, that a number or a date estimated to fit paints without an ellipsis, and that `####` fills its cell, is not checked for this version: the macOS widths are measured and held by tests, but the check on a Mac was not run ([Columns](exgrid/columns#declaring-columns)) (FN-12e, ADR-0016).
- **ExGrid:** on macOS, that the Command key is the Primary Modifier on every key path, the editor's included, so that Cmd+Enter fills, is not checked in a browser for this version: its tests without a browser pass, but the browser check on a Mac was not run ([On a Mac](reference/keyboard#on-a-mac)) (KB-3, ADR-0012).
- **ExSheet:** `NPV` is not supported and is `#NAME?`, as `IRR`, `XIRR` and `RATE` are: its last digits differ from Excel's, and no evaluation found gives every result Excel was seen to give ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `PMT`'s payment can differ from Excel's in its last digits: 23 of the 30 payments Excel was asked differ from ExSheet's by 1 to 11 units in the last place, a relative difference of at most about 1.3e-15, which at two of them shows in the 15th significant digit; and where the evaluations Excel's payments leave part, `PMT` is `#VALUE!` ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `INDIRECT` over a Linked Table's name on its own, `INDIRECT("Positions")`, is `#VALUE!`, where Excel reads the table's data: a Linked Table is read by key, never by position ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** A Formula ExSheet refused whose array's shape could depend on a Value, such as a refused `FILTER` or `=LEN(A1:A3)`, spills nothing, and the cells its array would cover read as blank ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** An italic number over `####` widens its column only to the width the same number takes upright, because ExSheet judges italic by the regular widths (ADR-0071); Excel's Font tab widened column A to 12.45 for italic, against 11.73 for a single underline or a colour, over 123456789.12 in `0.00` ([Cell Formats](exsheet/formatting#cell-formats)).
- **ExPivot:** with no report shown — only report filters placed, or a details tab over the report — the letters typed straight after Enter on a report filter, while its Filter… opens, are dropped with every key after them rather than typed into its search; Escape typed then still closes it. With the report shown they are typed into the search ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane)) (ADR-0061, ADR-0202).


## ExGrid

- **Added:** `ShapedGrid` builds a grid's columns at run time from a source's shape and a grid view's JSON spec (`GridViewSpec`), with no row type to write: hand its `Columns`, `Source` and `PinnedColumnCount` to an `ExGrid` of `TRow="ShapedRow"`. A decimal or double column must carry an Excel format code such as `#,##0.00`, and one left out is refused naming the column; integers and dates have fixed defaults. Each row is its own Row Key, so handing some of `Rows` back to `Source.ReplaceAll` narrows the grid and keeps the sort and filter the user set (ADR-0342).
- **Added:** `SelectionAfterInputsAsync` gives the selection the grid holds once it has answered every input it received so far. A control outside the grid that acts on the selection reads it there: `SelectionChanged` is raised after the render that paints it, a round trip later on the Server host, so a button pressed straight after a click would otherwise act on the selection before it (ADR-0240).
- **Added:** `ShowViewWithMatch(view, match)` places a single cell as `ShowViewWith(view, range, focus)` does, under the view's Row Sequence Version. If the find panel is open when the version is taken, that cell becomes the current match, marked apart, and Replace writes it. A Consumer that finds a match among rows it shows next, as ExSheet does on another Sheet, hands it this way, and gives the step up by throwing `OperationCanceledException` from its `OnFind` answer. `ForgetCurrentMatch()` drops the current match, for a Consumer that writes cells behind the grid's back, so the next Replace finds before it writes (ADR-0332).
- **Added:** `ShowViewWith(view, range, focus)` hands a place with the view a Consumer shows other rows under: the range is placed under the same Row Sequence Version, and the Focus is scrolled into view from the view's offsets, as `PlaceSelectionAsync` places among the rows already shown (ADR-0331).
- **Added:** `ExGrid.ReadView()` gives the Selection, with the Row Sequence Version it is written in, and the scroll offsets as a `GridView`; `ShowViewWith(view)` hands one back ahead of the version it is written in, and the grid takes both in the render that takes that version, in place of the empty Selection a new version leaves. A Consumer that shows one set of rows after another in one grid keeps each set's view with them. Handed from the handler of one of the grid's inputs, the inputs after it in the Input Order are taken under the new version, though on Blazor Server they told the browser's paint before it (ADR-0331).
- **Added:** A paste now says it is under way from its start until it lands or is refused: the root carries `aria-busy`, a bar across its top edge and a progress pointer paint it (`--ex-pasting-bar`, `--ex-pasting-bar-height`, `--ex-pasting-cursor`), and the live region says so once. Escape pressed meanwhile cancels the paste: before its Edit Intent is raised it is refused with the new reason `PasteRefusalReason.Cancelled`, and after, it stands and the grid says so. A Consumer that switches over `PasteRefusalReason` without a default case gains one value to handle (ADR-0240).
- **Added:** A grid built at run time from a shape can fetch its rows from a server a page at a time: `ShapedGrid.Fetch` takes a grid view's spec and a fetch that answers the grid's query with a `ShapedPage`, the rows as a Snapshot. A page whose columns are not the spec's fails its fetch, naming the input, and a date column with no format shows its time there. `FetchingGridSource.Requery` asks again from the top when state of the Consumer's own that the fetch reads has changed, and `ShapedRow.Page` is the Snapshot a row came back in (ADR-0343).
- **Added:** `JoinSurfaceAsync(element, around, escape)` puts a surface a design system draws outside the grid's Input Root — a `MudDialog`, a popover at the page's body — in the grid's Input Order until you dispose of what it returns: its claimed clicks, presses, submissions and keys are handled in their place, the keys typed in it while an earlier input is answered go, in order, to where that input left the keyboard, and an Escape no control of it keeps runs `escape` in its turn. A claim can name one key (`data-ex-claims="keydown:Enter"`). `HandKeyboardToFrameAsync(element)` has the keyboard wait on an empty element of your frame's own while the frame is on its way, so a dialog that gives the keyboard back as it closes gives it to nothing ([Appearance](exgrid/appearance); ADR-0240).
- **Added:** An edit open as `ShowViewWith` shows other rows stays open with the rows it was opened among: its editor keeps the keyboard unseen, the Formula Bar shows its text, Point points at the rows shown, and its commit raises `OnEdit` with the row it was opened on; a view read of its rows, handed back, brings the editor back over its cell. `ReadEditText()` reads the open edit's text from a handler (ADR-0331).
- **Added:** `HandKeyboardToAsync(control, preventScroll)` hands the keyboard to a control of yours as `HandKeyboardToAsync(control)` does, with the control taking it where it stands: nothing scrolls to bring it into view. ExSheet's Sheet Tabs use it, so the arrow keys on the tab list move along the tabs without scrolling the page, as they did before the tabs joined the Input Order ([Appearance](exgrid/appearance); ADR-0240).
- **Added:** Ctrl+H opens the find panel with Replace where the Consumer passes the new `OnReplace` and a visible column is Editable (in both Chromes, `ExGrid.MudBlazor` included); elsewhere the key is taken from the browser, opens nothing and raises `OnFindRefused` with the new `ReplaceUnavailable`, so ExSheet and ExPivot, which do not offer Replace yet, now keep Ctrl+H from the browser's history. Replace writes the current match as one ordinary `OnEdit` Edit Intent and finds the next; Replace All raises one `GridReplaceRequest`, which the Consumer performs all or nothing and answers with `GridReplaceResult.Replaced` (the count) or `Refused` (the cells and why), under the Row Sequence Version it read its rows in. `InMemoryGridSource.Replace` answers it for `GridSource.From`, and `GridReplace.Plan` is the reference implementation. `FindOutcome` gains `Replaced` and `ReplaceRefused`, `FindRefusalReason` gains `ReplaceUnavailable` and `ReplaceRefused`, and `FindContext` gains the Replace part a substituted find panel draws (ADR-0332).
- **Added:** While the find panel holds a search, every matching cell on screen is marked (`ex-match-mark`), and the current match apart (`ex-match-mark-current`), in the Visual Tokens `--ex-match-mark-fill` and `--ex-match-mark-current-fill`; rows scrolled into view are marked as they are painted, and the marks clear when the panel closes or its text is emptied. With `GridSource.From` the reference rule marks; a Consumer that answers Find itself (`OnFind`, `GridSource.Fetch`'s `find`, or a Source of its own) passes the rule it finds by as the new `FindMatch` parameter (`FindMatchOf<TRow>` over a `GridFindPattern`), and without one nothing is marked. `GridFind.Matches` is the reference rule for one cell, and `GridFindRequest.Pattern` a step's text and options (ADR-0332).
- **Added:** `ExGrid.InOrderAsync(handling)` runs work of your own that no control numbered, such as a command from a button outside the grid, at its place in the Input Order: after every input received so far, with the inputs after it waiting until it ends, and never taking a number told for a control's event. `ExGrid.CancellableInOrderAsync(handling, cancelledTooLate)` does the same for work that may take a while, such as reading a file: from the call until it ends the Settled Stamp names it (`data-ex-work-placed`) and the Settled Mark is off, and once its turn starts the root carries `aria-busy` and `data-ex-work` and is painted with the `--ex-pasting-*` tokens. A plain Escape typed in the grid against a page whose stamp named the work cancels its token, whether the work still waits for its turn or runs, and does nothing else; one typed against a page that did not yet name it is an ordinary key, decided by what it was typed against and not by when it arrives; an Escape that came after the work landed, or found it over otherwise, runs `cancelledTooLate` in its own turn with whether it had landed. Both may be called from any thread; disposing of the grid cancels the work under way, and one cancellable work inside another is refused. While such work waits or runs, the Settled Stamp no longer says that an Escape leaves the grid (ADR-0240).
- **Added:** A control outside the grid's own markup — a Chrome's, a popover's contents, a command of your own beside the grid — can now take its place in the grid's Input Order: call `InOrderAsync(kind, handling)` on the grid, or the new `InOrder` delegate on `GridPopoverContext`, with the `GridInputKind` of the event it answers. - **Added:** An Observation's `Edges` join its nodes by their Row Keys, drawn as lines between them, for a lattice or a network. An edge to a key no node carries is refused. The CSV lists the edges after the nodes ([Colour, size and edges from columns](exchart/spec#colour--size-and-edges-from-columns); ADR-0321).
- **Added:** A chart saves its CSV, SVG and PNG on one click, from its toolbar, its context menu or `DownloadAsync`, and a 3D surface its SVG and PNG, turned as the user left it. The SVG is the drawing on screen with its stylesheet inside it, the PNG is painted from it at the Device Pixel, and both take the theme's colours as the screen shows them ([CSV, SVG and PNG](exchart/export#csv--svg-and-png); ADR-0282).
- **Added:** ExChart, line, step and point charts of the family's data drawn as SVG by Blazor, ships in three packages: `ExChart.Engine`, `ExChart` and `ExChart.MudBlazor`. Each series declares its Series Kind and is drawn as what it is; the chart has a tooltip, a legend that hides a series, panels, a dragged range raised as events, a summary read aloud and "View as table" ([Overview](exchart); ADR-0250).
- **Added:** `GridChartBridge` charts a grid's selection from the grid's context menu, and the chart follows the rows it was made from: a recalculated value is redrawn, a deleted row becomes a gap with a note, and a click on a point moves the grid's Focus to its cell ([From the grid](exchart/grid); ADR-0258).
- **Added:** `ExChart.MudBlazor` draws the chart's toolbar and context menu with MudBlazor's controls (`MudChartChrome`), places the charts made from a grid's selection in tabs beside or below the grid (`MudChartDock`), and gives the theme's Primary, Secondary and Tertiary to the first three series ([Charting a selection](exchart/grid#charting-a-selection); ADR-0281).
- **Added:** `ExChart` reads a Chart Spec through Chart Sources itself: given `Spec` and `Sources` in place of a `Definition`, it asks for the device pixels of its width, and again whenever the range changes, keeping what it shows, Busy, until the newest ask is answered. An overtaken answer is dropped, and one from older data is said in the chart's place. `ExChart.Definition` is now nullable, so code that reads it back sees a nullable reference (ADR-0322, ADR-0344).
- **Added:** A 3D surface draws its colour bar beside the plot, in its SVG and PNG too, so each colour reads back as a value. `SurfaceDefinition.Centre` makes the colours diverge from a value such as 0 for a P&L: red below, blue above, as deep on one side as on the other at the same distance, through the `--ex-chart-diverge-0` to `-8` Visual Tokens ([Coloured from a centre](exchart/surface#coloured-from-a-centre); ADR-0310).
- **Added:** A surface's axes may place their values in proportion or by their logarithm (`SurfaceAxis.Scale`), and its values may be on a logarithmic scale (`SurfaceDefinition.ZScale`), which stays proportional with a Note when a value is at or below zero. An axis left as it was keeps its values evenly spaced, as before ([Logarithmic axes](exchart/surface#logarithmic-axes); ADR-0312).
- **Added:** `SurfaceDefinition.FromTriangles` draws a surface on the application's own triangle mesh, such as a finite element solution, as its triangles: never resampled onto a grid. The tooltip reads a node, and a click slices through it, cutting the triangles exactly and saying it reads between nodes linearly on them ([On your own triangles](exchart/surface#on-your-own-triangles); ADR-0311).
- **Added:** An Observation's marks take their colour (`ColourBy`, in nine heat bands, or diverging from a centre) and their size (`SizeBy`, area in proportion) from a column, with a key beside the plot and in the saved files. A point with no value is drawn as an outline and counted in the Notes; the tooltip and the CSV carry the values ([Colour, size and edges from columns](exchart/spec#colour--size-and-edges-from-columns); ADR-0320).
- **Fixed:** `ExSurface.SvgTextAsync` hands over a drawing of any size on Blazor Server. The text crossed the circuit as one message, and a drawing over the circuit's 32 KB message limit closed the circuit instead of answering. It now crosses as a stream (ADR-0282).
- **Fixed:** Under MudBlazor, the Chart Filters row lines up: every field is one width with its label on the outline, Apply is a filled primary button as tall as the fields and on their baseline, and the axis switch stands a little apart, since it takes effect at once. Before, a date picker left a wide gap after itself, From and To showed their labels as placeholders beside a labelled Axis, and Apply sat lower and shorter than the fields.
- **Fixed:** Quotes and the curve declared `Interpolated` that share their colour key, in one panel, now show one legend entry, as ADR-0257 says, instead of one each. Clicking it hides both, and showing it again shows both; `OnSeriesVisibilityChanged` and `HiddenSeries` name the quotes' identity. Two quote series of one colour key keep an entry each. `ChartScene.LegendMembers` says which series an entry stands for (ADR-0257).

## ExBoard

- **Added:** A chart over an input held on a server is drawn: the Board asks the source's `BoardSource.Thin` for 2,048 thinned pixels, again when a link narrows the chart or its range is narrowed. A link from a pivot over such an input asks `BoardSource.Under` for the values under the selected cells (`BoardSource.ValuesUnder` is the reference), through `BoundBoard.SelectCellsAsync`. Both were refused before; a source without `Thin` or `Under` is still refused at binding, by path. `BoundBoard.SelectCells` throws for a pivot over a server (ADR-0344, ADR-0345).
- **Added:** An input too large to download is bound to a `BoardSource` in the component's `Sources`, or in `BoundBoard.BindAsync`. The Board asks the source for its shape and checks it before anything is drawn. A grid's rows are then fetched a page at a time, and each fetch, like each report source the source makes for a pivot, is handed the conditions the Board's links narrow the view by (`BoardQuery.Narrowing`), to execute where the data lives. A selection reaching rows not fetched narrows nothing and is said under the grid (ADR-0343).
- **Added:** ExBoard ships as a package of its own: a Board, JSON an AI composes once and a workflow runs without AI, laid out as rows of ExGrid, ExPivot and ExChart views over typed Board Inputs. `BoardSpec.Read` reads the Board's JSON, a public surface from this version, and refuses what it cannot draw by its path; `<ExBoard>` draws it, with links, outputs on a confirm and the user's changes as a Board Diff; `BoardSpec.Tool` and `BoardSpec.Answer` are the `draw_board` tool. ExBoard takes the products' Chromes, so it has no MudBlazor Wrapper (ADR-0340).

## Data

- **Added:** `BoardInput` names a piece of data and declares each column's kind; `Check` and `Bind` hold a source's `DataShape` against it and refuse a missing column, or one of another kind, naming the input and the column. `FromJson` makes a Snapshot from JSON rows by the declared kinds, and refuses a value that does not convert by its row and column rather than drop it or read it as zero. Refusals are a `BoardRefusedException` carrying the path (ADR-0341).
- **Added:** A source held on a server says its shape, its columns' names and kinds, before any row. `FetchingGridSource.Shape`, `FetchingPivotReportSource.Shape` and `SnapshotArrow.ReadShapeAsync`, which reads an Arrow stream's schema alone, answer it through `IShapedSource.ShapeAsync`; a pivot source over a Snapshot answers the Snapshot's own. `BoardInput.CheckAsync` checks an input against it and refuses a source that says none (ADR-0341).
- **Added:** A new package, `ExGrid.Data.Xlsx`, reads and writes the xlsx file format with nothing but the platform's zip and XML: `XlsxReader` streams a workbook's sheets, shared strings, Formulas (shared and array ones included), column widths, styles and theme colours in batches, with every worksheet element it does not read reported as an `XlsxElement` for the caller to judge, yielding and reporting progress, and `XlsxWriter` writes the same model back as a valid package. A file it cannot read faithfully is refused by name as an `XlsxException` naming its reason, part, sheet and cell: not a zip, macro-enabled, binary, legacy, encrypted, a DTD, a part that is not well-formed or cannot be inflated, a cell written twice, out of order or naming a style the file does not hold, or beyond `XlsxReadOptions.MaxUncompressedBytes` (1 GiB) or `MaxCells` (ten million) by default (ADR-0330).
- **Added:** `XlsxWriteOptions.MaxUncompressedBytes` bounds what an `XlsxWriter` writes: the package's parts together may hold at most this many uncompressed bytes, by default `XlsxReadOptions.DefaultMaxUncompressedBytes` (1 GiB), the most a file is read with, so what is written opens again. A write that would go past it is refused as an `XlsxException` with `XlsxRefusal.TooLarge`, naming the part and the sheet it met, rather than failing later with an unnamed `IOException` (ADR-0330).
- **Added:** `ExGrid.Data.Xlsx` writes an Excel Table: `XlsxSheetWriter.AddTable(XlsxTable)` adds one over a sheet's cells before its header's row, with its table part, the sheet's relationship and `tableParts`, its content type, an AutoFilter and Excel's default table style. The writer refuses a name Excel does not give a Table, one another table or defined name of the workbook has, overlapping tables, and a header whose cells do not hold the columns' names as text (ADR-0330).
- **Added:** `ExGrid.Data.Xlsx` is published with the rest of the family, at its one version: it reads and writes the `.xlsx` format on a server with no component (ADR-0042).
