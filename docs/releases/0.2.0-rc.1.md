## Highlights

- The first release candidate of 0.2.0, against which the Definition of Done is signed off. What the version holds is in the notes of [0.2.0-beta.1](release-notes#0-2-0-beta-1) and [0.2.0-beta.3](release-notes#0-2-0-beta-3), and React starts at [React](getting-started/react) (ADR-0205).
- Three things 0.1.0 left out are in: Find All, Excel's list of every match, in ExGrid, ExSheet and ExPivot ([Sorting, filtering and find](exgrid/sorting-filtering#find-all); ADR-0470); a Reference Outline dragged by its edge or a corner while a Formula is edited ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#completion--hints-and-pointing); ADR-0480); and a band down the Focus's column ([Selection and keyboard](exgrid/selection#which-row-and-column-am-i-on-); ADR-0008).

## Breaking changes

- **ExGrid:** `GridSource.Fetch` (and `FetchingGridSource`'s constructor) take a new optional `findAll` parameter at the end. Source that calls it compiles unchanged; a library compiled against the old signature is rebuilt ([Sorting, filtering and find](exgrid/sorting-filtering#find-all); ADR-0470).
- **ExGrid:** `GridFindWords` has a new required `FindAll` member, the Find All button's words: a Chrome that builds one sets it, or it no longer compiles (CS9035); a Chrome that reads `GridFindView.Words` changes nothing ([Sorting, filtering and find](exgrid/sorting-filtering#find-all); ADR-0470).

## Known limitations

- **ExGrid:** under a Windows display language other than English and Japanese, a number or a date can come out cut at its column's edge rather than as `####`, because `system-ui`'s widths were measured under those two only ([Columns](exgrid/columns#declaring-columns)) (FN-12e, ADR-0016).
- **ExSheet:** `NPV` is not supported and is `#NAME?`, as `IRR`, `XIRR` and `RATE` are: its last digits differ from Excel's, and no evaluation found gives every result Excel was seen to give ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `PMT`'s payment can differ from Excel's in its last digits: 23 of the 30 payments Excel was asked differ from ExSheet's by 1 to 11 units in the last place, a relative difference of at most about 1.3e-15, which at two of them shows in the 15th significant digit; and where the evaluations Excel's payments leave part, `PMT` is `#VALUE!` ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** `INDIRECT` over a Linked Table's name on its own, `INDIRECT("Positions")`, is `#VALUE!`, where Excel reads the table's data: a Linked Table is read by key, never by position ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** A Formula ExSheet refused whose array's shape could depend on a Value, such as a refused `FILTER` or `=LEN(A1:A3)`, spills nothing, and the cells its array would cover read as blank ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#supported-functions)).
- **ExSheet:** An italic number over `####` widens its column only to the width the same number takes upright, because ExSheet judges italic by the regular widths (ADR-0071); Excel's Font tab widened column A to 12.45 for italic, against 11.73 for a single underline or a colour, over 123456789.12 in `0.00` ([Cell Formats](exsheet/formatting#cell-formats)).
- **ExPivot:** with no report shown — only report filters placed, or a details tab over the report — the letters typed straight after Enter on a report filter, while its Filter… opens, are dropped with every key after them rather than typed into its search; Escape typed then still closes it. With the report shown they are typed into the search ([The PivotTable Fields pane](expivot/layout#the-pivottable-fields-pane)) (ADR-0061, ADR-0202).

## ExGrid

- **Added:** The find panel gains Find All, Excel's list of every match with its exact count, where the source answers it: `GridSource.From` does, `GridSource.Fetch` takes a `findAll` delegate, and an `IGridSource` answers `CanFindAll` and `FindAllAsync` (in React, `findAll` beside `find`). A line chosen moves the Focus there ([Sorting, filtering and find](exgrid/sorting-filtering#find-all); ADR-0470).
- **Added:** A grid that outlines a Formula's References takes `moveReference` (Blazor: `MoveReference`, a script function): with it, an outline's edges and corner squares can be dragged to move or resize the Reference, and the grid writes the text the function answers for the range reached ([Overview](exgrid#a-grid-drawn-in-the-browser); ADR-0480).
- **Added:** `highlightFocusColumn` (`HighlightFocusColumn` in Blazor) paints a band down the Focus's column, beneath the Selection, as `highlightFocusRow` does across its row; with both on they cross as Excel's Focus Cell does. Its colour is `--ex-focus-column-fill`, which is `--ex-focus-row-fill` unless set, so every Wrapper's palette already covers it ([Selection and keyboard](exgrid/selection#which-row-and-column-am-i-on-); ADR-0008).

## ExSheet

- **Added:** The find panel gains Find All: within the Sheet it lists the Sheet shown, and within the Workbook every Sheet in tab order, each line named `Sheet!A1`; a line on another Sheet shows that Sheet with the cell selected ([Workbook](exsheet/workbook#find-and-replace); ADR-0470).
- **Added:** While a Formula is edited, a Reference Outline can be dragged as in Excel: by its edge to move the Reference, and by the square at each of its corners to resize it. The Formula is rewritten in the Reference's own form (`$A$1` moved is `$C$1`), and Escape during the drag puts the Reference back ([Formulas](exsheet/formulas#completion--hints-and-pointing); ADR-0480).

## ExPivot

- **Added:** The report's find panel gains Find All, which lists every match over the report as shown, with the exact count, and moves the Focus to a line chosen ([Layout](expivot/layout#find); ADR-0470).
