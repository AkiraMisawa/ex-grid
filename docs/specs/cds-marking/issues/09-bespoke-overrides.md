# 09: Overrides of Bespoke Proxies

Status: ready-for-agent

**What to build:** the day's Overrides of a Bespoke Proxy (spec, "Bespoke Proxies").

- **The list's dialog:** a level, an offset or T-1 for a Point, with a reason, applied after the
  proxy is evaluated. It is painted Modified.
- **Apply for today** (ticket 08): that day's Entries replace the definition's in the evaluation.
- **Lifetime:** both end with the day, and both are recorded.
- **Sensitivity:** a Point whose Override leaves it reading nothing has no sensitivity, and the
  readiness panel warns.
