Package validation of the ten packages against 0.1.0-beta.2 (ADR-0201)

Date: 2026-10-12, on Linux (a cloud container), .NET SDK 10.0.203 through nix.
Branch claude/public-surface at 03e5a938, the baseline commit. Each package was packed once, in the
order tests/ExGrid.PackageSmoke/check.sh packs them, with:

  dotnet pack src/<Package> -c Release -o <feed> -p:Version=0.1.0-pv \
    -p:EnablePackageValidation=true -p:PackageValidationBaselineVersion=0.1.0-beta.2

The baseline packages came from nuget.org through the container's proxy. The two properties were
passed on the command line and are not committed: ADR-0201 runs this once, for 0.1.0's Release Notes,
and the PublicAPI files beside each project are the baseline from now on.

ExGrid was packed again the same way after the verifiers' read, the only package whose surface that
change moved. Its findings and its output below are that second run's. Fourteen more members of ExGrid
are internal, as the lists settled: GridMetrics' eleven layout numbers, GlyphWidthTable.TryGetWidthPx,
ReferenceColour.PaletteLength, InMemoryGridSource.DistinctValueCap and FetchingGridSource.DefaultPageRows.
CellTextMetrics.For is public again, as ADR-0016 names it. The other nine packages' runs are the
first ones, at 03e5a938; nothing in their surface has moved since.

merged on main after beta.2 rather than from ADR-0201's internalisation:
  surface-exgrid-interop.md: OnKeyAsync, OnKeyFieldTextAsync and OnPasteStreamsAsync take the paint;
  surface-exgrid-clipboardpayload.md: its constructor and Deconstruct take Landing;

    and EditDiscardReason's values, which moved down one when OrderChanged left.

internal now, and a row's values are read from PivotReportState.Rows.
